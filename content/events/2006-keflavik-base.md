## Summary
NATO's history of Iceland says a permanent US defence force was hosted at Keflavík until 2006, and that since 2006 there has not been a permanent foreign military presence in the Keflavík area. The page says Iceland continues to operate the NATO radar installations and integrated system from Keflavík and provides host-nation support to NATO Allies. It does not print the day the base closed.
