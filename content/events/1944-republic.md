## Summary
On 17 June 1944 the Republic of Iceland was formally proclaimed at Þingvellir. The Government of Iceland's brief history dates the proclamation to that day and places it after the 1918 sovereignty arrangement, under which Iceland was united with Denmark under the Danish crown. NATO's history of Iceland says Iceland gained independence from Denmark on 17 June 1944.

The constitution now published by the Government is the Constitution of the Republic of Iceland, No. 33, 17 June 1944, as amended on 30 May 1984, 31 May 1991, 28 June 1995, and 24 June 1999. Article 1 says Iceland is a republic with a parliamentary government. Article 2 says Althingi and the President jointly exercise legislative power. This is not the 1874 constitution → [[1874-constitution]]. The parliament is [[althingi]].
