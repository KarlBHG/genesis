# Iceland joins the European Economic Area

On 1 January 1994 the EEA Agreement entered into force. Iceland participated with Norway. The Government of Iceland dates Liechtenstein's full participation to 1 May 1995, so this entry does not say Liechtenstein joined on the same day. The agreement gave Iceland access to the EU single market while fisheries and agriculture stayed outside it → [[eea-agreement]].

**What changed:**
- Free movement of goods, services, capital, and people with the EU
- Obligation to adopt EU regulations in covered areas
- End of some trade tariffs

**Long-term impact:**
- Iceland's economy becomes deeply integrated with the EU
- Regulatory alignment becomes a permanent feature of governance
- Sets the stage for later Schengen accession → [[schengen]]

The EEA relationship depends on EFTA membership → [[efta]].
