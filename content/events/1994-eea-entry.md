# Iceland joins the European Economic Area

On 1 January 1994 the EEA Agreement entered into force. Iceland participated with Norway. The Government of Iceland dates Liechtenstein's full participation to 1 May 1995, so this entry does not say Liechtenstein joined on the same day. The foreign-affairs page says the agreement brought free movement of goods, services, capital, and persons. It does not say fisheries and agriculture stayed outside the agreement → [[eea-agreement]].

**What changed:**
- Free movement of goods, services, capital, and people with the EU
- EEA-relevant EU legislation is incorporated into the agreement by the EEA Joint Committee

**Long-term impact:**
- Iceland's economy becomes deeply integrated with the EU
- Regulatory alignment becomes a permanent feature of governance
- Sets the stage for later Schengen accession → [[schengen]]

The EEA relationship depends on EFTA membership → [[efta]].
