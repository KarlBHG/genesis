## Summary
NATO's history of Iceland says that in 1951 Iceland signed a bilateral defence agreement with the United States, allowing the US military to create the Iceland Defense Force at Keflavík Base near Reykjavík. The same page says Iceland was perturbed by the upheaval of the Korean War. It gives the year and not a day. The treaty entry is [[nato]]. The foreign ministry is [[ministry-for-foreign-affairs]]. The page dates the end of a permanent US force at Keflavík to 2006, again without a day → [[2006-keflavik-base]].
