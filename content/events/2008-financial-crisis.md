# 2008 financial crisis

This entry is dated 23 October 2008 because that is the day the IMF says its staff discussions with the Icelandic authorities ended. It is not a claim that the banking system failed on that single day.

The IMF staff report completed on 15 November 2008 describes a banking crisis of extraordinary proportions, and says the virtual collapse of the on-shore foreign exchange market was an immediate risk because of Iceland's import dependence. The authorities then requested a Stand-By Arrangement → [[imf-program-2008]].

The central bank and the finance ministry are the domestic institutions this map links to that request → [[sedlabanki]] → [[ministry-of-finance]].
