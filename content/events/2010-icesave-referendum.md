## Summary
On 6 March 2010 Iceland held a national referendum on Act 1/2010, which amended Act 96/2009 and empowered the Minister of Finance, on behalf of the Treasury, to guarantee loans to the Deposit and Investor Guarantee Fund from the British and Dutch governments to cover minimum deposit guarantees to depositors in Icesave accounts of Landsbanki Íslands hf. The Government's release of that day calls it the first national referendum since the foundation of the Republic. It says the President withheld confirmation of the amendment on 5 January, and that early figures indicated a decisive result, so that Act 1/2010 would be repealed under Article 26 of the Constitution. The release does not print a yes or no percentage. The parliament is [[althingi]]. The bank named in the release is [[landsbankinn]]. The later referendum is [[2011-icesave-referendum]].
