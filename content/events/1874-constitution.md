## Summary
The Government of Iceland's national-anthem page says King Christian IX of Denmark presented Iceland with a constitution during his visit for the 1874 millennium celebrations, and that the constitution entailed substantial improvements to Iceland's legal status. The embassy history page says that in 1874, when Iceland celebrated the millennium of the first settlement, it received a constitution from the Danish king and control of its own finances. Neither page prints a day for the presentation. The anthem page dates a different event on that visit: the first performance of the national anthem, at a service in Reykjavík Cathedral on 2 August 1874, in the king's presence.

The anthem page places this constitution before home rule in 1904, sovereignty in 1918, and the republic on 17 June 1944 → [[1944-republic]]. It is not the constitution now in force. The legislature is [[althingi]].
