## Summary
The Government of Iceland's brief history says that in the year 930, at the end of the Settlement period, a constitutional law code was accepted and the Alþingi was established. The same account says its judicial power was distributed between four local courts and a court held annually at the national assembly at Þingvellir. The parliament's own entry is [[althingi]].

The page gives the year and not a day.
