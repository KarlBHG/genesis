## Summary
A Government statement dated 10 April 2011 says a referendum on the Icesave agreement was held on Saturday, 9 April. It concerned Act No. 13/2011, which authorised the Minister of Finance to sign agreements guaranteeing reimbursement to the United Kingdom and the Netherlands of costs from minimum guarantees to depositors in branches of Landsbanki Íslands hf., and payment of the remaining balance and interest. The statement says Alþingi adopted the Act by 44 of 63 members on 16 February 2011, and that the President refused to sign it on 20 February 2011. It says the Act was rejected by a majority of voters and is therefore repealed, that turnout was high, and that close to 40% voted for and 60% voted against. The earlier vote is [[2010-icesave-referendum]]. The EFTA Court case that followed is [[2013-icesave-judgment]]. The bank is [[landsbankinn]].
