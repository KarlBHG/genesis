## Summary
A Government of Iceland release dated 1 April 2009 says that on 28 November the previous year Alþingi approved amendments to the Foreign Exchange Act intended to prevent a major depreciation of the króna. The release says the Act gave the Central Bank of Iceland temporary authorisation to set rules limiting international capital movements, with the aim of restricting the outflow of foreign currency, for instance by imposing obligations to repatriate foreign currency. The crisis entry is [[2008-financial-crisis]]. The Bank is [[sedlabanki]]. The later easing is [[2017-capital-controls]].
