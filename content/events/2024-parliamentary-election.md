## Summary
Landskjörstjórn's notice on Ísland.is, dated 10 December 2024, says the commission allocated parliamentary seats that day after the general election to Alþingi held on 30 November 2024. The notice says the election was held on Saturday, 30 November 2024. The parliament is [[althingi]].
