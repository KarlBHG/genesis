## Summary
On 16 July 2009 Alþingi approved a proposal on an application for membership of the European Union. The parliament's vote record for that sitting, at 14:00, says the proposal was approved, with 33 votes in favour, 28 against, and 2 members not voting. The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says Iceland applied for EU membership on 17 July 2009 with a formal letter from the Prime Minister and the Minister for Foreign Affairs. The same ministry page says that in June 2010 the European Council unanimously agreed to start accession negotiations and to grant Iceland candidate status. It does not print a day in June. The legislature is [[althingi]]. The ministry is [[ministry-for-foreign-affairs]]. The EEA relationship is [[eea-agreement]]. The 2015 letter about candidate status is [[2015-eu-candidate-letter]].
