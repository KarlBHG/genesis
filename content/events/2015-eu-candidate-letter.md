## Summary
On 12 March 2015 the Minister for Foreign Affairs, Gunnar Bragi Sveinsson, delivered a letter to the Latvian foreign minister, Edgars Rinkēvičs, then holding the Presidency of the Council of the European Union, and to the Commission. The Government's release of that day says the cabinet had decided at its meeting the previous Tuesday that it did not intend to restart accession negotiations. The release says the Government therefore considered that Iceland was no longer a candidate country, and asked the EU to act accordingly. It does not print the Tuesday's date.

A later Ministry for Foreign Affairs page, dated 22 June 2026, says the March 2015 letter spoke of stopping the accession negotiations completely and of the government's position that Iceland should not be considered a candidate country. The same page says two parliamentary resolutions for a formal withdrawal of the membership application were submitted to Alþingi, and that neither was passed nor processed. It says a Commission spokesperson said on 15 January 2025 that the application was still considered valid because it had never been formally withdrawn, and that Ursula von der Leyen reconfirmed on 17 July 2025, while visiting Iceland, that the EU considered the application valid. This entry does not call the 2015 letter a withdrawal. The application vote is [[2009-eu-application]]. The ministry is [[ministry-for-foreign-affairs]]. The EEA Agreement, which the 2015 release says cooperation should continue to rest on, is [[eea-agreement]].
