# Retail

## Summary
Hagar hf. is a listed retail group. Its consolidated financial statements for the year ended 28 February 2025 say amounts are in ISK million and print sales of 180.342 for 2024/25. The English management presentation for that year describes Bónus and Hagkaup as chains inside the group → [[hagar]].

Statistics Iceland’s release on the 2024 income statement and balance sheet says revenue in wholesale and retail trade and repair of motor vehicles contracted by 19.6% in 2024. That is one industry line for the business economy. It is not Hagar’s sales. The release says figures for 2024 are preliminary. It does not print a retail turnover level.
