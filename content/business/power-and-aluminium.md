# Power and aluminium

## Summary
Landsvirkjun’s 2025 annual report describes the company as an energy company owned by the Icelandic nation and as a public partnership owned by the Icelandic State. The same report says it produces more than 70% of all electricity generated in Iceland and supplies industry, services, and households, from 14 hydropower stations, three geothermal stations, and two research wind turbines.

The consolidated Act on Landsvirkjun, as read on Alþingi’s statute page, is more precise about the ownership shares. Article 1, citing Act No. 154/2006, says the Treasury holds 99.9% and names a 0.1% holder. The act prints that holder once as “Eignarhluta ehf.” and once as “Eignarhlutir ehf.” This entry does not identify that company further → [[landsvirkjun]].

The Minister of Finance appoints the board → [[ministry-of-finance]]. Customer categories and the dated power contracts are on [[landsvirkjun-power-sales]].

Landsvirkjun’s 2024 annual report says the company’s own electricity generation that year was 14,171 GWh. A key-figure line in the same report prints “14,2 TWst”, down 3.8%. Those are the company’s figures, not a total for Iceland. The report says the company generates more than 70% of all electricity in Iceland, that about 85% of its energy is sold on the large-user market, and that 15% is sold on the wholesale market. The 2025 financial statements say about 80% of the company’s generation is sold to energy-intensive customers and about 20% to domestic electricity sales companies. The 85% line and the 80% line are different years and different wordings. Neither is a national share for power-intensive industry.

The same 2024 report says installed capacity in Iceland is about three gigawatts, or 3,000 megawatts, and footnotes Orkustofnun’s electricity statistics page. It does not print a national generation total in terawatt-hours. The statistics page lists spreadsheets. This entry does not add those files up.

Norðurál Grundartangi’s 2024 annual report says operating revenue was USD 793 million, and that Landsvirkjun is one of three power suppliers to the smelter → [[nordural]] → [[landsvirkjun]]. The Straumsvík smelter is [[isal]]. The Reyðarfjörður smelter is [[alcoa-fjardaal]].

In the 2024 goods-trade release, aluminium and aluminium products were 33% of the total export value. The category table does not publish a billion-ISK figure for that line, and it does not split the line by smelter → [[aluminium-exports-2024]] → [[goods-exports-2024]].
