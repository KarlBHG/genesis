# Tourism

## Summary
The government tourism page says tourism has in recent years become one of the main pillars of the Icelandic economy. The page header places the topic under the Ministry of Industries. The body of the same page still says the Department of Tourism at the Ministry of Culture and Business Affairs develops official tourism policy, and that the Icelandic Tourist Board, the Tourist Site Protection Fund, the Route Development Fund, and the Tourism Task Force operate under the auspices of the ministry. It also says the ministry has agreements with Business Iceland on promotion of Iceland as a tourism destination.

That older ministry name is not the name on the current cabinet list. This entry does not assign the tourism portfolio to a named minister beyond what those two pages say.

Statistics Iceland’s services release for 2024 values travel-service exports at an estimated 447.8 billion ISK, up 4% from 2023 → [[services-trade-2024]].
