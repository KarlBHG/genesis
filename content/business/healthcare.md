# Healthcare

## Summary
The Ministry of Health page says Alma D. Möller has served as Minister of Health since 21 December 2024 → [[alma-d-moller]] → [[ministry-of-health]].

The 2024 budget note for hospital services says activity in that policy area is the responsibility of the Minister of Health. It says specialized hospital services are in the hands of the health ministry, Landspítali, and Sjúkrahúsið á Akureyri. Landspítali’s own history line describes the institution as a modern university hospital → [[landspitali]].

One public spending figure is printed in the government release of 20 December 2023, after Alþingi approved the 2024 budget. Contributions to health affairs are about 379.4 billion ISK, and the release calls that the largest expenditure item in the budget. The same release says appropriations for construction of the new Landspítali are 19.3 billion ISK. Those are budget figures for 2024, not a later outturn → [[althingi]].

The 2026 budget law prints a separate health-ministry authorisation. Article 4’s row for Heilbrigðisráðuneytið is 437.690,0 m.kr., and hospital services inside it are 192.293,9 m.kr. That is not a restatement of the 379.4 billion ISK health-affairs line → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
