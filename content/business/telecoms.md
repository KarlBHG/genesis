# Telecoms and media

## Summary
Fjarskiptastofa supervises electronic communications under Act No. 75/2021 → [[fjarskiptastofa]]. The companies below publish their own accounts. Síminn’s 2024 consolidated statements print total revenue of ISK 27.832 million → [[siminn]]. Sýn hf., which sells services under the Vodafone brand, prints revenue from goods and services of 21.647 millj. kr. in 2024 → [[syn]]. Nova Klúbburinn hf. prints total revenue of 13.314 milljónum króna in 2024 → [[nova]].

Ríkisútvarpið is the state-owned public-service broadcaster already on this map → [[ruv]].
