# Construction and housing

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s release on the 2024 income statement and balance sheet says that, of the country’s main industries, revenue increased most in real estate (14.7%) and construction (11.0%), and that 14% growth was in construction of buildings. It also says operating expenses in construction increased by 11.7%, in line with revenue growth. Figures for 2024 are preliminary. Inflation in 2024 was 5.8% on the annual average consumer price index. The release does not print a construction turnover level or an employment count.

Íbúðalánasjóður was dissolved at the end of 2019. ÍL-sjóður holds the tasks, rights, duties, and assets that did not move to Húsnæðissjóður and Húsnæðis- og mannvirkjastofnun. The half-year accounts to 30 June 2024 say the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs has oversight of ÍL-sjóður → [[il-sjodur]] → [[ministry-of-finance]]. Félags- og húsnæðismálaráðuneytið administers housing → [[ministry-of-social-affairs-and-housing]]. Those accounts do not place ÍL-sjóður under that ministry.
