# Banking after 2008

## Summary
Landsbankinn’s 2025 factbook says Landsbankinn hf. was established on 7 October 2008, and that the history of its predecessor runs back to 1886. The fact sheet dated 31 December 2025 says the Icelandic State holds 98.2% of the shares → [[landsbankinn]] → [[landsbankinn-state-holding]]. The ministry that controls the State’s holding is [[ministry-of-finance]].

Íslandsbanki’s 2025 annual report says the Icelandic State completed the sale of its remaining 45.2% shareholding in 2025, that the sale was in May, and that the bank had more than 25,000 shareholders at year-end 2025 and had moved fully into private ownership → [[islandsbanki]].

Arion Bank’s 2025 report says the bank’s assets were ISK 1,755 billion at year-end. The same report says capital requirements are set by the Financial Supervisory Authority of the Central Bank → [[arion-banki]] → [[sedlabanki]].

The wider crisis and the IMF stand-by are already mapped at [[2008-financial-crisis]] and [[imf-program-2008]]. Supervision sits with [[sedlabanki]] and [[financial-supervisory-authority]] from 1 January 2020. This entry does not list Arion’s shareholders.
