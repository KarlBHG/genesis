# Goods imports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s goods-trade release of 14 May 2025 says the value of imported goods in 2024 amounted to 1,356.7 billion ISK cif (1,286.1 billion ISK fob). The same release says: “The largest trading countries in imports of goods in 2024 were Norway, Germany, the United States and China. The value of imports from countries within the EEA amounted to 60% of all imports in 2024.”

The release does not print a separate value for Norway, Germany, the United States, or China, and it does not name a company as the seller. The export total and the export partners are on [[goods-exports-2024]]. The EEA arrangement is [[eea-agreement]].

Commodity rows already followed from the same table are [[food-and-beverages-imports-2024]], [[fuels-and-lubricants-imports-2024]], and [[industrial-supplies-imports-2024]].
