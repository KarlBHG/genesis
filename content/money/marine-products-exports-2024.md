# Marine products exports, 2024

## Summary
The goods table in Statistics Iceland’s 14 May 2025 release, titled “The value of exports and imports in 2023 and 2024” and measured in billion ISK at the current exchange rate, lists marine products exports in 2024 at 346.5 billion ISK fob. That is 36.1% of goods exports, down 1.8% from 352.8 billion ISK in 2023. The release text rounds the same share to 36% and the decline to 2%.

Within marine products, the release says fresh fish was 27% and frozen fillets 25%. Farmed fish is not inside this row. The same table lists farmed fish separately, at 53.8 billion ISK fob in 2024 (5.6% of goods exports).
