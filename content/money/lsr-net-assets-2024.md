# LSR net assets, 2024

## Summary
LSR’s English Overview 2024 says amounts are in millions of ISK. Net assets for pension payments at year-end 2024 were 1,566,743.9 million ISK for the fund as a whole. Division A was 1,302,404.4, Division B 233,166.6, and Division S (voluntary savings) 31,172.8. The same line for 2023 was 1,405,194.6 million ISK.

This is one fund, Lífeyrissjóður starfsmanna ríkisins, not a total for every Icelandic pension fund → [[lsr]].
