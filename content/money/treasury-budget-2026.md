# Treasury budget for 2026

## Summary
These are budgeted figures for the Treasury, Part A1, in the 2026 budget law, not a later outturn. Article 1 prints total revenue of 1.591.246,2 m.kr. and total expenditure of 1.606.582,9 m.kr. The same article prints an overall balance of -15.336,6 m.kr. The unit heading on that table is m.kr., million krónur. The Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs prepares the budget → [[ministry-of-finance]]. Alþingi passed it → [[althingi]].

## Approval
The ministry’s 2026 budget page says the finance minister introduced the bill on 9 September 2025 and that Alþingi passed it as law on 18 November 2025. A ministry release dated 19 December 2025 also says Alþingi has approved the 2026 budget. Both dates are on official pages.

## Balance, as two official pages print it
Article 1 of the budget law also prints an operating balance of 1.098,0 m.kr. and a primary balance of 77.013,5 m.kr. Interest expense in that article, Vaxtagjöld, is 144.259,4 m.kr.

The 19 December 2025 release does not print total revenue or total expenditure. It says the overall Treasury deficit is expected to be just over 28 billion ISK (the release prints “rúmlega 28 ma.kr.”), or 0.5% of GDP, in 2026. It says the primary balance, defined there as the balance excluding interest income and interest expense, is positive by about 67 billion ISK, or 1.3% of GDP, and that the interest balance is negative by just over 95 billion ISK, or 1.8% of GDP. Those release figures do not match Article 1’s overall balance of -15.336,6 m.kr. or its primary balance of 77.013,5 m.kr. The interest balance in the release is also a different line from Article 1’s interest expense. Both pages are quoted here. Neither figure is adjusted to the other.

Article 2 is a separate cash statement for the same year. It prints collections of 1.480.464,9 m.kr., payments of 1.506.444,6 m.kr., and cash from operations of -25.979,7 m.kr. Those are not the Article 1 totals.

## Expenditure lines the law breaks out
Article 4 sets authorisations by policy area and ministry. Its table heading is also m.kr. The column used below is the total authorisation, heildarfjárheimild. Article 3’s policy-area table is not used: its hospital-services row prints 1.692.125,0, which cannot sit in the same million-króna unit as Article 1’s total expenditure of 1.606.582,9.

The 35 policy areas, the work their ministry reports describe, and every Article 4 row are on [Budget](/money#government-programs). Rows that share a name sit under different ministries. They are not added together.

Health sits under Heilbrigðisráðuneytið. That ministry row prints a total authorisation of 437.690,0 m.kr. Inside it, hospital services (Sjúkrahúsþjónusta) are 192.293,9 m.kr., health services outside hospitals are 102.779,1 m.kr., nursing and rehabilitation are 86.038,7 m.kr., and medicines and medical devices are 45.269,9 m.kr. The healthcare page records a different figure: the 20 December 2023 release, after the 2024 budget was approved, says contributions to health affairs are about 379.4 billion ISK and calls that the largest expenditure item in that budget. That 2024 line is not restated in the 2026 articles opened here → [[healthcare]].

Social protection is not one row. Under Félags- og húsnæðismálaráðuneytið, disability and disabled people’s affairs print 132.621,8 m.kr. and affairs of older people print 127.566,1 m.kr. That ministry’s own total authorisation is 393.868,7 m.kr. and includes other areas, so it is not a social-protection total. Nursing also appears again under that ministry, at 7.175,2 m.kr., separate from the health ministry’s nursing row.

Education is not one row. Under Mennta- og barnamálaráðuneytið, upper-secondary education prints 53.311,4 m.kr. Under Menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðuneytið, higher education prints 72.938,4 m.kr. A further higher-education row under Atvinnuvegaráðuneytið prints 232,0 m.kr. Those rows are not added together here.

Debt service is also more than one line. Article 1’s interest expense is 144.259,4 m.kr., as above. Article 4, under Vaxtagjöld ríkissjóðs, prints a total authorisation of 125.388,6 m.kr. for financial cost (Fjármagnskostnaður) and a Treasury contribution of 89.909,1 m.kr. on that row. The December release’s interest balance, just over 95 billion ISK, is the third presentation.

## Borrowing authorisation
Article 5 authorises the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs, on behalf of the Treasury, to borrow up to 290 billion ISK, up to 78.7 billion ISK toward the Central Bank’s equity, and up to a further 140 billion ISK. The same article authorises smaller on-lending lines. Those caps are permissions in the 2026 law, not the outstanding stock → [[treasury-debt]].
