# Industrial supplies imports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s 2024 goods table calls this row “Industrial supplies n.e.s.” Imports were 371.9 billion ISK cif, up 9.6% from 339.2 billion ISK in 2023, and 27.4% of goods imports. It is the largest import row in that table.

The release text says the largest part of import value was raw and consumable goods, at 27%. This entry does not treat that sentence and the table row as proven to be the same basket beyond both being the largest share in the same release, at 27% and 27.4%.

Other published import rows in the same table, not given their own pages, are capital goods except transport (358.1 billion ISK cif, 26.4%), transport equipment (169.5, 12.5%), consumer goods n.e.s. (178.8, 13.2%), and goods n.e.s. (0.6, 0.0%). The import total is on [[goods-exports-2024]].
