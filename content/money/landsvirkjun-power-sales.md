# Landsvirkjun power sales

## Summary
Landsvirkjun’s customers page says the company sells to power-intensive industries and lists the categories Aluminium Smelters, Ferrosilicon Plants, Data Centres, Other Industries, and Electricity Retailers. The page also gives an approximate split of sales. That split is not dated on the page, so the percentage is left out.

One contract the company has published is dated. On 23 June 2026 Landsvirkjun said it and Borealis Data Center had signed an agreement for an additional 12 MW of firm power, on top of existing contracts, for the data centre in Blönduós. The same notice says Borealis has been a customer since it began operations in Iceland in 2018 → [[borealis-data-center]] → [[landsvirkjun]].

Named aluminium-smelter contracts were not in the customers-page text opened for this entry.
