# Landsvirkjun power sales

## Summary
Landsvirkjun’s customers page says the company sells to power-intensive industries and lists the categories Aluminium Smelters, Ferrosilicon Plants, Data Centres, Other Industries, and Electricity Retailers. The page also gives an approximate split of sales. That split is not dated on the page, so the percentage is left out.

Three smelter contracts are dated on pages opened for this entry.

On 15 February 2021 Landsvirkjun said it and Rio Tinto Iceland had amended the 2010 power purchase agreement for the Straumsvík smelter. The notice says the contract specifies the sale of 390 MW or “3.416 GWh pr. year” and expires in 2036. The energy figure is quoted as printed → [[isal]].

Alcoa Corporation’s 2025 annual report says Landsvirkjun supplies the Fjarðaál smelter from a hydroelectric facility under a 40-year power contract that expires in 2048, with a price renegotiation effective from 2028. The report does not print a megawatt figure for that contract → [[alcoa-fjardaal]].

On 24 March 2026 Landsvirkjun said it and Norðurál signed an agreement for Grundartangi from the beginning of 2027 through the end of 2033, for up to 250 MW a year. The notice says the first agreement was in 1997 for approximately 100 MW, and that sales in recent years have exceeded 200 MW → [[nordural]].

On 23 June 2026 Landsvirkjun said it and Borealis Data Center had signed an agreement for an additional 12 MW of firm power, on top of existing contracts, for the data centre in Blönduós. The same notice says Borealis has been a customer since it began operations in Iceland in 2018 → [[borealis-data-center]] → [[landsvirkjun]].
