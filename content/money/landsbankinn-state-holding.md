# State holding in Landsbankinn

## Summary
Landsbankinn’s factbook labels its fact sheet 31 December 2025. That sheet says the Icelandic State holds 98.2% of the shares and that shareholders number approximately 819. The bank’s ownership page says shareholders are around 800, that the National Treasury is the largest owner of a 98.2% share, and that the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs controls the State’s holding → [[ministry-of-finance]] → [[landsbankinn]].

The factbook PDF’s document properties record a creation date of 29 January 2026. The ownership page itself does not print a year next to the 98.2% figure. The dated figure used here is the one on the 31 December 2025 fact sheet.
