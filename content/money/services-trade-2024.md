# Services trade, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s services release of 5 September 2025 says that in 2024 exports of services were 982.7 billion ISK and imports were 712.5 billion ISK. The surplus was 270.2 billion ISK, compared with 325.9 billion ISK in 2023 at current exchange rates.

Travel services were the strongest-growing export category in that release, valued at an estimated 447.8 billion ISK, an increase of 4% from 2023. Service exports to the United States were 315.3 billion ISK. The release says the headline for service exports was virtually unchanged from 2023, down 0.3 billion ISK. A heading says imports of services increased by 12% between years. The next sentence says the value of service imports was 55.4 billion ISK higher than in 2023, representing an 8% increase at current exchange rates. The 55.4 billion figure is that increase, not the import total of 712.5 billion ISK. A later sentence says imports from the United States rose 12%. The 12% heading and the 8% sentence are both on the release. This entry does not pick one.
