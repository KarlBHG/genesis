# Services trade, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s services release of 5 September 2025 says that in 2024 exports of services were 982.7 billion ISK and imports were 712.5 billion ISK. The surplus was 270.2 billion ISK, compared with 325.9 billion ISK in 2023 at current exchange rates.

Travel services were the strongest-growing export category in that release, valued at an estimated 447.8 billion ISK, an increase of 4% from 2023. Service exports to the United States were 315.3 billion ISK. The release says the headline for service exports was virtually unchanged from 2023, down 0.3 billion ISK, while one sentence in the same release describes the import increase as 12% and another as 8% (55.4 billion ISK). Both wordings are on the release; this entry does not pick one.
