# Agricultural products exports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s 2024 goods table lists agricultural products exports at 8.8 billion ISK fob, the same figure as in 2023, and 0.9% of goods exports. The change column is −0.1%.

This is the export row, not a measure of what was produced at home. Home meat production for the same year is on [[agriculture]]. Food brought in is [[food-and-beverages-imports-2024]]. The parent goods total is [[goods-exports-2024]].
