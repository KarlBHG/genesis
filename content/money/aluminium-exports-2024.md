# Aluminium exports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s goods release of 14 May 2025 says that in 2024 the export of aluminium and aluminium products was 33% of the total goods-export value, and that this was the largest part of industrial-product exports. The same release says manufacturing products were 54% of goods exports. The accompanying table puts manufacturing products at 513.8 billion ISK fob, 53.6% of exports.

The table does not break aluminium out in billion ISK. This entry does not multiply 33% by the 959.0 billion total. The parent total is [[goods-exports-2024]]. The sector that generates the power for this industry is [[power-and-aluminium]].
