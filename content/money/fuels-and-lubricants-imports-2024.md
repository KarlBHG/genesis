# Fuels and lubricants imports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s 2024 goods table lists fuels and lubricants imports at 156.1 billion ISK cif, down 9.8% from 173.1 billion ISK in 2023, and 11.5% of goods imports. The release text says the value of fuels and lubricants decreased by 10% between those years. The table’s −9.8% is the figure used here.

The parent import total, 1,356.7 billion ISK cif, is on [[goods-exports-2024]].
