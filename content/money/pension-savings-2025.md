# Pension savings, year-end 2025

## Summary
On 25 June 2026, Fjármálaeftirlit Seðlabanka Íslands published a summary of pension-fund annual accounts and comparable figures from custodians of personal pension savings. The release says the summary is for 2025. It says 19 pension funds operated in 25 mutual divisions, and that 13 of those funds offered personal savings in 45 divisions. Other domestic custodians of personal savings were five, in 31 divisions. Three foreign custodians offered products classified as personal pension savings.

The same release says pension savings of the population, in mutual and personal form, held by pension funds and other custodians, were 9,463 billion ISK at year-end and had risen by 8% from the year before. The release prints “9.463 ma.kr”. It does not call that figure the total assets of the pension funds alone. This page does not add the fund entries below into that total.

Individual funds already on the map are [[lsr]], [[bru]], [[birta]], and [[stapi]]. LSR’s own 2024 net assets are a separate line → [[lsr-net-assets-2024]]. The publisher is part of [[sedlabanki]] → [[financial-supervisory-authority]].
