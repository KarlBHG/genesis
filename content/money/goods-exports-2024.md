# Goods exports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s goods-trade release of 14 May 2025 says that in 2024 Iceland’s goods exports were 959.0 billion ISK fob. Goods imports were 1,356.7 billion ISK cif (1,286.1 billion ISK fob). The fob/cif deficit was 397.7 billion ISK.

The table in that release, “The value of exports and imports in 2023 and 2024”, is in billion ISK. Export rows for 2024:

- Marine products, 346.5 billion ISK fob, 36.1% → [[marine-products-exports-2024]] → [[fisheries-and-quotas]]
- Agricultural products, 8.8 billion ISK fob, 0.9% → [[agricultural-products-exports-2024]] → [[agriculture]]
- Farmed fish, 53.8 billion ISK fob, 5.6%, a separate row from marine products
- Manufacturing products, 513.8 billion ISK fob, 53.6%. The release text says manufacturing products were 54% of exports, and that aluminium and aluminium products were 33% of total export value → [[aluminium-exports-2024]] → [[power-and-aluminium]]
- Other products, 36.0 billion ISK fob, 3.8%

The largest goods-export partners were the Netherlands, the United States, and the United Kingdom. The release says 77% of all exports went to EEA countries → [[eea-agreement]].
