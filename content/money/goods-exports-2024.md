# Goods exports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s goods-trade release of 14 May 2025 says that in 2024 Iceland’s goods exports were 959.0 billion ISK fob. Goods imports were 1,356.7 billion ISK cif (1,286.1 billion ISK fob). The fob/cif deficit was 397.7 billion ISK.

Manufacturing products were 54% of goods exports. Aluminium and aluminium products were 33% of total export value. Marine products were 36% of total goods exports. Within marine products, fresh fish was 27% and frozen fillets 25%.

The largest goods-export partners were the Netherlands, the United States, and the United Kingdom. The same release says 77% of all exports went to EEA countries → [[eea-agreement]].
