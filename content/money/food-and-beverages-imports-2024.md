# Food and beverages imports, 2024

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s 2024 goods table calls this row “Food and beverages”, not “food and agricultural products”. Imports were 121.7 billion ISK cif, up 3.1% from 118.0 billion ISK in 2023, and 9.0% of goods imports (1,356.7 billion ISK cif).

The same table’s export row for agricultural products is much smaller → [[agricultural-products-exports-2024]]. The sector page is [[agriculture]]. The goods total, which also states the import total, is [[goods-exports-2024]].
