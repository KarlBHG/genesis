# Utanríkisráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page is the Ministry for Foreign Affairs. The Icelandic page is headed Utanríkisráðuneytið. Þorgerður Katrín Gunnarsdóttir took office as Minister for Foreign Affairs on 21 December 2024. The Icelandic page says the same date.

The English page says the ministry safeguards access to international markets and free trade, supports Icelandic firms abroad, promotes Icelandic arts and culture, conducts political relations with other states and international organisations, including human rights, security and defence, and trade, runs international development cooperation, and assists Icelandic citizens abroad. The Icelandic page describes the same foreign service.

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 39.526,8 m.kr. for Utanríkisráðuneyti → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
