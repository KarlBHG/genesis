# Landbúnaðarháskóli Íslands

## Summary
The consolidated Act on public universities, No. 85/2008, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, names this university in Article 1 among those “sem reknir eru sem opinberir háskólar og lúta yfirstjórn ráðherra.” A bare reference to the minister in that act means the Minister of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education → [[ministry-of-culture-innovation-and-higher-education]].

Article 8 says the minister appoints the university rector for five years on the nomination of the university council. Article 24 says each university has its own appropriation in the budget law.

The 2026 budget report for policy area 21 prints one task line naming this university and one of the few amounts in that table: “Bygging jarðræktarmiðstöðvar við Landbúnaðarháskóla Íslands á Hvanneyri.” The responsibility column prints “Menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðuneyti og atvinnuvegaráðuneyti” and the funding column prints “100 m.kr.” The report’s list of changes to the subcategory’s authorisation repeats the amount without the university’s name: “Fjárheimild málaflokksins er aukin um 100 m.kr. vegna byggingar jarðræktarmiðstöðvar á Hvanneyri, sbr. markmið nr. 2.” → [[policy-area-21-haskolastig]] → [[treasury-budget-2026]].

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints the rows for policy area 21 at the subcategory level only and does not name this university.

The university’s own site calls it “Háskóli lífs og lands”, prints the address Hvanneyri – 311 Borgarbyggð and kennitala 411204-3590, and lists degree programmes in búvísindi, hestafræði, náttúru- og umhverfisfræði, skipulagsfræði, skógfræði, landslagsarkitektúr, endurheimt vistkerfa, and búfræði. The agriculture sector page is [[agriculture]]. The other public universities with records here are [[haskoli-islands]] and [[haskolinn-a-akureyri]].
