# Fjármálaeftirlitið

## Summary
Fjármálaeftirlitið, the Financial Supervisory Authority, is part of Seðlabanki Íslands. Act No. 92/2019 says the bank undertakes the tasks entrusted to the Financial Supervisory Authority, and that the authority is part of the bank. Article 47 transfers those tasks to the central bank when the act enters into force on 1 January 2020.

This entry does not give a separate founding year for the old authority. The act that was opened states the transfer, not the authority's original statute.

Decisions that law assigns to the Financial Supervisory Authority are taken by the Central Bank or its Financial Supervision Committee → [[sedlabanki]].
