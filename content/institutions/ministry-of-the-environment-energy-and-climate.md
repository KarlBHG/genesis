# Umhverfis-, orku- og loftslagsráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page is the Ministry of the Environment, Energy and Climate. The Icelandic page is headed Umhverfis-, orku- og loftslagsráðuneytið. Jóhann Páll Jóhannsson became minister on 21 December 2024. The Icelandic page says the same date, and that he has sat in parliament for the Social Democratic Alliance, for Reykjavík North, since 2021.

The English page says the ministry formulates and enforces environmental policy and supervises nature, conservation and outdoor recreation, national parks, climate, energy, animal protection, wildlife, cultural heritage, pollution and fire prevention, weather forecasting and avalanche protection, surveying and cartography, and environmental monitoring. The Icelandic page says its role is the framework for environmental protection and sustainable use of natural resources, with the tasks defined in the presidential decree on the division of ministerial affairs.

Umhverfisstofnun is not a current agency on the government’s institutions list. Its former site says it was wound up on 31 December 2024. The institutions list places Umhverfis- og orkustofnun and Náttúruverndarstofnun under this ministry → [[umhverfis-og-orkustofnun]] → [[natturuverndarstofnun]].

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 52.044,4 m.kr. for Umhverfis-, orku- og loftslagsráðuneyti → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
