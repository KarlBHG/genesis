# Republic of Iceland

## Summary
The Constitution of the Republic of Iceland, No. 33, 17 June 1944, as amended, says in Article 1: “Iceland is a republic with a parliamentary government.” Article 2 says Althingi and the President of Iceland jointly exercise legislative power, that the President and other governmental authorities referred to in the Constitution and elsewhere in the law exercise executive power, and that judges exercise judicial power. The proclamation of the republic is [[1944-republic]]. The parliament is [[althingi]]. The courts entry is [[courts]]. A company-registry search for the name “Stjórnarráð Íslands” returned “Leit eftir „Stjórnarráð Íslands“ skilaði engri niðurstöðu”. The Treasury is a separate registry record, legal form K1 Ríkisstofnun, not a limited company → [[rikissjodur]].

The about-the-government page says cabinet meetings are attended by all government ministers, and that the Prime Minister directs those meetings, as a rule twice weekly during the parliamentary session → [[prime-ministers-office]].

Act No. 92/2019, in the consolidated text of 1 September 2026, says Seðlabanki Íslands is an independent institution owned by the state → [[sedlabanki]].
