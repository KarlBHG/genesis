# United States

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s goods-trade release of 14 May 2025 names United States as an import and an export partner and does not print a separate value or a selling company. The sentence is: “The largest trading countries in imports of goods in 2024 were Norway, Germany, the United States and China.”


NATO’s history of Iceland says Iceland signed a bilateral defence agreement with the United States in 1951, and that a permanent US defence force was hosted at Keflavík until 2006. Iceland continues to operate the NATO radar installations there and provides host-nation support to NATO Allies.
