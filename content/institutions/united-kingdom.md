# United Kingdom

## Summary
Statistics Iceland’s goods-trade release of 14 May 2025 names United Kingdom as an export partner and does not print a separate value or a selling company. The sentence is: “Largest trading countries in export of goods were the Netherlands, the United States and the United Kingdom while 77% of all exports went to EEA countries.”
