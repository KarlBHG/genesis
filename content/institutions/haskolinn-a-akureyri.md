# Háskólinn á Akureyri

## Summary
The consolidated Act on public universities, No. 85/2008, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, names this university in Article 1 among those “sem reknir eru sem opinberir háskólar og lúta yfirstjórn ráðherra.” A bare reference to the minister in that act means the Minister of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education. The government’s institutions list also names Háskólinn á Akureyri, places it under Menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðuneytið, and gives the website unak.is → [[ministry-of-culture-innovation-and-higher-education]].

Article 8 says the minister appoints the university rector for five years on the nomination of the university council. Article 24 says each university has its own appropriation in the budget law and that the minister makes proposals for the contributions to each university.

The 2026 budget report for policy area 21 does not name this university in its task table, and Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints the rows for that policy area at the subcategory level only. No funding row naming this university is recorded here → [[policy-area-21-haskolastig]].

The university’s own site says the university council holds an open annual meeting at which the university’s activity and finances are presented, and that the annual report is published only in electronic form. The site prints the address Norðurslóð 2, 600 Akureyri and kennitala 520687-1229. The other public universities with records here are [[haskoli-islands]] and [[landbunadarhaskoli-islands]].
