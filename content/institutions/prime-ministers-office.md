# Forsætisráðuneytið

## Summary
The English page calls this the Prime Minister’s Office. The Icelandic ministry page is headed Forsætisráðuneytið. Kristrún Frostadóttir has been Prime Minister since 21 December 2024 → [[kristrun-frostadottir]].

The English page says the office supports the Prime Minister in administering the division of responsibilities between other ministries, coordinates government policy where issues meet, organises committees the Prime Minister appoints, and monitors implementation of government decisions. The Icelandic page says its tasks cover the constitution of the republic and the government as a whole, the cabinet and ministerial committees, the Council of State, economic affairs, legislative matters, the general conduct of government, and national symbols, as set out in the presidential decree on the division of ministerial affairs.

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 5.448,3 m.kr. for Forsætisráðuneyti. The unit heading is m.kr. → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
