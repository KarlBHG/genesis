# Náttúruverndarstofnun

## Summary
The agency’s site says it began work on 1 January 2025 and took over nature protection, wildlife and hunting management from Umhverfisstofnun, and the operation of Vatnajökulsþjóðgarður. It says the agency handles administration, supervision, and other tasks in nature protection and sustainable development, protected areas, protection of wild birds and mammals, and hunting management. It also says Iceland has about 130 protected areas, including three national parks, and names Vatnajökulsþjóðgarður and Snæfellsjökulsþjóðgarður.

The government’s institutions list places Náttúruverndarstofnun under Umhverfis-, orku- og loftslagsráðuneytið and gives the website nattura.is → [[ministry-of-the-environment-energy-and-climate]].

Umhverfisstofnun’s former site says that agency was wound up on 31 December 2024 → [[umhverfis-og-orkustofnun]].
