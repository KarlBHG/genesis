# Héraðsdómstólarnir

## Summary
The consolidated Act on the courts, No. 50/2016, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 3: “Dómstólar í héraði eru átta.” The same article names each court, its seat, and the municipalities in its jurisdiction, and says the district of each district court shall be one thinghá. The appeal court above them is [[landsrettur]] and the highest court is [[haestirettur]].

The eight courts and the seats the article prints for them:

- Héraðsdómur Reykjavíkur, seated in Reykjavík
- Héraðsdómur Vesturlands, seated in Borgarnes
- Héraðsdómur Vestfjarða, seated in Ísafjörður
- Héraðsdómur Norðurlands vestra, seated in Sauðárkrókur
- Héraðsdómur Norðurlands eystra, seated in Akureyri
- Héraðsdómur Austurlands, seated in Egilsstaðir
- Héraðsdómur Suðurlands, seated in Selfoss
- Héraðsdómur Reykjaness, seated in Hafnarfjörður

This map keeps the eight as one record because the statute and the budget law treat them as one appropriation. Article 7 says the budget law sets out separately the appropriations to Hæstiréttur Íslands, Landsréttur, the district courts jointly, and the judicial administration, and that the board of the judicial administration divides between the district courts the money granted to them in one sum in the budget: “Stjórn dómstólasýslunnar skiptir á milli héraðsdómstólanna fé sem skal veitt þeim í einu lagi með fjárlögum.” The same article says that board decides the number of district judges and other staff at each district court → [[domstolasyslan]] → [[policy-area-02-domstolar]].

Article 29 says there are 42 judges in the districts and that the minister appoints them to the office of district judge for an indefinite term: “Dómarar í héraði eru 42 að tölu og skipaðir ótímabundið í embætti héraðsdómara af ráðherra.” A bare reference to the minister in that act means the Minister of Justice → [[ministry-of-justice]].
