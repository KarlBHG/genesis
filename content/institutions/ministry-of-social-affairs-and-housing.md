# Félags- og húsnæðismálaráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page is the Ministry of Social Affairs and Housing, and it says the ministry was established on 15 March 2025. The Icelandic page is headed Félags- og húsnæðismálaráðuneytið. Ragnar Þór Ingólfsson has been Minister of Social Affairs and Housing since 11 January 2026. The English minister page says he has been a member of parliament since 2024, for the People’s Party. Inga Sæland held this office from 21 December 2024 until 11 January 2026. That earlier term is on her education-ministry page → [[ministry-of-education-and-children]].

The English page says the ministry administers social affairs, social security, and housing, and lists disability affairs, employment, family affairs, immigrants, housing, and planning. The Icelandic page lists social and family affairs, social-security pensions, labour affairs, affairs of older people, and housing and planning. Both lists are printed. They are not the same list.

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 393.868,7 m.kr. for Félags- og húsnæðismálaráðuneyti. That is the ministry row, not a social-protection total → [[treasury-budget-2026]].

ÍL-sjóður’s half-year accounts to 30 June 2024 say the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs has oversight of the fund that kept the residual book of Íbúðalánasjóður. They do not place ÍL-sjóður under this ministry. Construction and housing figures are on [[construction-and-housing]] → [[il-sjodur]].
