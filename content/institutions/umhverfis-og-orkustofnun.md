# Umhverfis- og orkustofnun

## Summary
The government’s institutions list places Umhverfis- og orkustofnun under Umhverfis-, orku- og loftslagsráðuneytið and gives the website uos.is → [[ministry-of-the-environment-energy-and-climate]].

The agency’s own site says Umhverfisstofnun was formally wound up at the end of 2024, and that this site carries the material on chemicals, supervision, marine and water affairs, the circular economy, permits, air quality, and climate. The same site says Orkustofnun was formally wound up at the end of 2024, and points to material on natural resources and the energy transition. Umhverfisstofnun’s former site says that agency was wound up on 31 December 2024, and that Umhverfis- og orkustofnun and Náttúruverndarstofnun began work on 1 January 2025 → [[natturuverndarstofnun]].

The site does not print an English name for the agency.
