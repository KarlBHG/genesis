# Samband íslenskra sveitarfélaga

## Summary
The English page calls this the Icelandic Association of Local Authorities, the forum for co-operation between local authorities. It was established in 1945 by 52 local authorities, and since 1973 every local authority in the country has been a member. The same page says the association is the joint representative of the country’s local authorities under the Local Government Act, No. 138/2011, and that it works with the government and Alþingi. Local government sits with the Ministry of Infrastructure → [[ministry-of-infrastructure]].

The English page says that in the year 2000 there were 124 municipalities, and that in May 2024 the number was 63. The Icelandic page on the municipalities says that after the elections of 16 May 2026 there are 61. Both sentences are on the association’s site. The Icelandic page also says there were 229 municipalities in 1950, and that the number has since fallen by 168.

The English page names the chairman as Jón Björn Hákonarson and the CEO as Arnar Þór Sævarsson. It says the national congress meets every year and elects an executive board of 9 representatives. The capital municipality is [[reykjavikurborg]].
