# Seðlabanki Íslands

## Role
Iceland's central bank. Responsible for monetary policy, financial stability, currency issuance, and foreign exchange reserves.

The bank's own account says it is an independent state-owned institution under the Prime Minister, and that the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs also handles financial stability and financial markets → [[ministry-of-finance]]. Act No. 92/2019 says the bank shall promote price stability, financial stability, and sound and secure financial activities, and that it maintains international reserves and payment intermediation.

## Powers
- The Monetary Policy Committee sets the policy interest rate
- Issues the króna (ISK)
- Manages foreign currency reserves
- Supervises payment systems, and carries out financial supervision → [[financial-supervisory-authority]]
- Lender of last resort

## Governance
Under Act No. 92/2019, as read in the English translation (the Icelandic text prevails):

- The Minister appoints the Governor for a five-year term, and three Deputy Governors for five-year terms. The bank's site says the institution comes under the Prime Minister.
- A Supervisory Board of seven members is elected by Alþingi → [[althingi]]
- Decisions on monetary-policy instruments are taken by the Monetary Policy Committee

An earlier draft of this entry described a three-member board of governors. That does not match Act No. 92/2019.

## Key constraints
- EEA membership does not require euro adoption → [[eea-agreement]]
- Must coordinate with IMF conditions (post-2008) → [[imf-program-2008]]
