# Seðlabanki Íslands

## Role
The English translation of Act No. 92/2019 says the Central Bank of Iceland is an independent institution owned by the State and operating under the auspices of the Minister. The translation says the Icelandic text prevails if they differ. The act does not name the minister’s portfolio in the articles opened here, and it does not give a founding year.

The bank’s about page, which is in Icelandic at the address on the English site, says Seðlabanki Íslands is an independent institution owned by the state. The same paragraph says the bank comes under the Prime Minister, and that the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs also handles financial stability and financial markets. That page and the act do not name the same oversight. Both stay → [[ministry-of-finance]].

Article 2 says the bank shall promote price stability, financial stability, and sound and secure financial activities, maintain international reserves, and promote a safe and effective financial system, including payment intermediation. The same article says the bank undertakes the tasks entrusted to the Financial Supervisory Authority, and that the authority is part of the bank → [[financial-supervisory-authority]].

## Debt management
The Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs debt strategy, issued 30 December 2024, says Government Debt Management is a department of this bank and executes the Treasury debt strategy under an agreement with the ministry. The stock and the ratings are on the debt page → [[treasury-debt]].

## Powers
- The Monetary Policy Committee takes decisions on interest rates, under Article 10
- Maintains international reserves
- Carries out financial supervision → [[financial-supervisory-authority]]

## Governance
Under Act No. 92/2019, as read in the English translation:

- The Minister appoints the Governor for a five-year term, and three Deputy Governors for five-year terms.
- A Supervisory Board of seven members is elected by Alþingi → [[althingi]]
- The Governor chairs the Monetary Policy Committee

Article 47 repeals Act No. 36/2001 and entered into force on 1 January 2020, when the tasks of the Financial Supervisory Authority transferred to the central bank.

No annual-account total for assets or staff was opened.
