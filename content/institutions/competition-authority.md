# Samkeppniseftirlitið

## Summary
The English site is titled The Competition Authority and says the authority plays a key role in ensuring effective competition in Icelandic markets. Its address on that page is Borgartún 26, 105 Reykjavík. The government’s competition page says Samkeppniseftirlitið carries out supervision and day-to-day administration under the competition act, and that its task is to work against unreasonable obstacles to business, harmful oligopoly and restraints of competition, and to ease the entry of new competitors. The government’s institutions list names Samkeppniseftirlitið, places it under Atvinnuvegaráðuneytið, and gives the website samkeppni.is.

Atvinnuvegaráðuneytið is the ministry already on the map as the Ministry of Industries. That Icelandic page says the ministry took up its present form on 15 March 2025 and that Hanna Katrín Friðriksson has been minister since 21 December 2024 → [[ministry-of-industries]].

A government page on competition still says the Minister of Tourism, Industry and Innovation is responsible for the competition act, and that the Minister of Culture and Business appoints the authority’s three-member board. Those are earlier ministerial titles. The institutions list is the page used here for the current ministry.
