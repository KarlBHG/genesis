# Mennta- og barnamálaráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page is the Ministry of Education and Children. The Icelandic page is headed Mennta- og barnamálaráðuneytið. Inga Sæland took office as Minister of Education and Children on 11 January 2026. The English minister page says she was Minister of Social Affairs and Housing from 21 December 2024 to 11 January 2026, and that she has been a member of parliament for Reykjavík South since 2017, for the People’s Party.

The English page says the main areas are education from preschool through upper secondary school, continuing and adult education, children, and sports. It says local authorities are responsible for preschools and primary schools. The Icelandic page also names child welfare and child protection, youth work, and arts schools and folk high schools.

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 73.572,4 m.kr. for Mennta- og barnamálaráðuneyti. That row is the ministry total, not a single education-policy total → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
