# Embætti landlæknis

## Summary
The Ísland.is page for Embætti landlæknis says the office grants health professionals licences to practise and issues certificates confirming a valid licence. The same page covers patient rights and complaints, quality and supervision of health services, public health and prevention, communicable diseases and vaccination, and health statistics. The address on the page is Katrínartún 2, 105 Reykjavík.

The government’s institutions list places Embætti landlæknis under Heilbrigðisráðuneytið and gives this Ísland.is address → [[ministry-of-health]] → [[healthcare]].

The page does not print an English name for the office.
