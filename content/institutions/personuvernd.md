# Persónuvernd

## Summary
The Ministry of Justice page on data protection says Persónuvernd is an independent institution that supervises the data-protection laws and rules made under them. The same page says Dómsmálaráðuneytið oversees data-protection policy, implementation, and the regulatory framework. The government’s institutions list places Persónuvernd under Dómsmálaráðuneytið and gives the website personuvernd.is → [[ministry-of-justice]].

The page does not print an English name for the institution.
