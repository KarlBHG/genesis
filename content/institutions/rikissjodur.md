# Ríkissjóður Íslands

## Summary
The company registry lists Ríkissjóður Íslands, kennitala 5402696459, registered 14 February 1969, at Katrínartúni 6, 105 Reykjavík. The legal-form cell prints “K1 Ríkisstofnun”. The activity line prints “84.11.0 Almenn stjórnsýsla og löggjöf”. That form is a state institution, not a hlutafélag.

This record is the Treasury. It is not the constitution’s description of the republic → [[republic-of-iceland]]. The finance ministry handles central-government debt → [[ministry-of-finance]].

The central-bank act says the Treasury is liable for all of the bank’s obligations: “Ríkissjóður ber ábyrgð á öllum skuldbindingum Seðlabankans.” The same article says the bank is owned by the state, “í eigu ríkisins”, which is a different sentence from the Treasury’s liability → [[sedlabanki]].
