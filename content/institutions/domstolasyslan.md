# Dómstólasýslan

## Summary
The consolidated Act on the courts, No. 50/2016, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 5: “Dómstólasýslan er sjálfstæð stjórnsýslustofnun sem annast sameiginlega stjórnsýslu dómstólanna.” The three tiers it administers are [[haestirettur]], [[landsrettur]], and [[heradsdomstolar]].

Article 6 says the minister appoints the five people who form the board: “Ráðherra skipar fimm menn sem mynda stjórn dómstólasýslunnar.” The same article says one of them is elected by the Supreme Court judges from among themselves and chairs the board, one by the Landsréttur judges, one by the district judges, and one by court staff other than judges, and that the minister appoints the fifth without nomination and that person shall not be an employee of the courts. A bare reference to the minister in the act means the Minister of Justice → [[ministry-of-justice]].

Article 7 sets out what the board does. It assesses and proposes to the minister the necessary appropriations for the judicial administration, Hæstiréttur and Landsréttur, and the joint appropriation for the district courts. It divides between the district courts the money granted to them in one sum in the budget. It decides the number of district judges and other staff at each district court and sets general rules on moving judges between them. It sets the rules on judges’ leave and grants that leave. The article also says the courts shall be defined as a separate policy area in the budget bill and in the budget law → [[policy-area-02-domstolar]].

Article 11 says the judicial administration nominates the third member of the five-member committee that assesses applicants for judicial office and that this member shall not be a sitting judge. The other four are nominated by Hæstiréttur, which supplies the chair, by Landsréttur, by Lögmannafélag Íslands, and by Alþingi.
