# EEA Joint Committee

## Summary
The foreign-affairs page on the EEA says that EEA-relevant pieces of legislation are incorporated into the EEA Agreement by decision of the EEA Joint Committee. The same page says the agreement extends the EU single market to Iceland, Norway, and Liechtenstein. It does not say the European Union governs Icelandic territory → [[eea-agreement]] → [[efta]].
