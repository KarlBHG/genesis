# Courts

## Summary
This is an index page. The courts named on it now have their own records: [[haestirettur]], [[landsrettur]], [[heradsdomstolar]], and the shared administration [[domstolasyslan]]. Read those for the statutory jurisdiction, appointment, and funding lines. The typed edges are stored on them, not here.

The courts portal says everyone has a right to a fair and public hearing before an independent and impartial court. Its section on the court system names Hæstiréttur, Landsréttur, the héraðsdómstólar, and Endurupptökudómur. The case-law search on the same portal covers judgments of Hæstiréttur, Landsréttur, the district courts, and Endurupptökudómur.

The government’s institutions list places these courts under Dómsmálaráðuneytið:

- Hæstiréttur Íslands, haestirettur.is
- Landsréttur, landsrettur.is
- Héraðsdómur Austurlands, Héraðsdómur Norðurlands eystra, Héraðsdómur Norðurlands vestra, Héraðsdómur Reykjaness, Héraðsdómur Reykjavíkur, Héraðsdómur Suðurlands, Héraðsdómur Vestfjarða, and Héraðsdómur Vesturlands, each with a page on heradsdomstolar.is

The Ministry of Justice about page says the courts and the justice system are among the ministry’s tasks. That is the link used here. The portal’s sentence on an independent court is also on the record → [[ministry-of-justice]].

Endurupptökudómur is named on the courts portal. The institutions list does not give it a row. Article 4 of the consolidated Act on the courts, No. 50/2016, says Endurupptökudómur operates under Chapter IX of that act and that the other courts are Félagsdómur and Landsdómur, governed by provisions in other acts. Those three have no record here yet.
