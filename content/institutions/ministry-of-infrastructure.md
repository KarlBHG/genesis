# Innviðaráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page is the Ministry of Infrastructure. The Icelandic page is headed Innviðaráðuneytið. Eyjólfur Ármannsson took office as Minister of Transport and Local Government on 21 December 2024, and became Minister of Infrastructure on 15 March 2025, when a presidential decree changed the title of the office. The Icelandic page says the same.

The English page says the responsibilities cover transport, local government, regional policy, telecommunications, and postal matters. The municipalities’ association is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Isavia’s domestic-airport agreement is with this ministry → [[isavia]]. It names the Electronic Communications Office of Iceland among the institutes in the ministry’s scope. The Icelandic page names transport, local government and regional affairs, and telecommunications and digital infrastructure → [[fjarskiptastofa]].

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 115.580,6 m.kr. for Innviðaráðuneyti → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
