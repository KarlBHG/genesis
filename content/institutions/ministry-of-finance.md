# Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs

## Summary
The ministry says its principal functions are the state's financial and economic affairs: policy, plans, and budgets, plus central-government operations and the state's assets and human resources. The page used here does not give a founding year, so none is stated.

Seðlabanki Íslands says this ministry handles financial stability and financial markets, while the bank itself comes under the Prime Minister → [[sedlabanki]].
