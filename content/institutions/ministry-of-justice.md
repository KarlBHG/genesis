# Dómsmálaráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page is the Ministry of Justice. The Icelandic page is headed Dómsmálaráðuneytið. Þorbjörg Sigríður Gunnlaugsdóttir assumed the office of Minister of Justice on 21 December 2024. The Icelandic page says the same date, and that she has been a member of parliament for Reykjavík North since 2020, for Viðreisn.

The English page says the ministry is responsible for the justice system and civil rights, including civil defence, public prosecution, the courts, immigration, enforcement of sentences, the coast guard, policing, equality and human rights, personal rights, district commissioners, and elections. The Icelandic page names public safety, the courts, policing, immigration, equality and human rights, legislative work, and children’s rights → [[courts]].

The government’s institutions list places Persónuvernd under this ministry → [[personuvernd]].

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 77.252,0 m.kr. for Dómsmálaráðuneyti → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
