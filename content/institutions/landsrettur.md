# Landsréttur

## Summary
The consolidated Act on the courts, No. 50/2016, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 2: “Landsréttur er áfrýjunardómstóll og hefur aðsetur í Reykjavík.” The header of the same act says the act took effect on 1 January 2018, apart from named transitional provisions that took effect on 14 June 2016. The court above it is [[haestirettur]] and the courts of first instance are [[heradsdomstolar]].

Article 21 says sixteen judges sit in Landsréttur and that the President of Iceland appoints them for an indefinite term on the minister’s proposal: “Í Landsrétti eiga sæti [16 dómarar] sem forseti Íslands skipar ótímabundið samkvæmt tillögu ráðherra.” The bracket in the statute collection marks text inserted by a later amending act. A bare reference to the minister in the act means the Minister of Justice → [[ministry-of-justice]]. Article 11 says one member of the committee that assesses applicants for judicial office is nominated by Landsréttur.

Article 7 says the budget law must set out separately the appropriations to Hæstiréttur Íslands, Landsréttur, the district courts jointly, and the judicial administration, and that the board of the judicial administration assesses and proposes to the minister the necessary appropriations for the judicial administration, Hæstiréttur and Landsréttur. Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints one row naming this court under Dómsmálaráðuneyti → [[policy-area-02-domstolar]] → [[domstolasyslan]].

The index page for the judiciary as a whole is [[courts]].
