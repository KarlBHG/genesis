# Háskóli Íslands

## Summary
The consolidated Act on public universities, No. 85/2008, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 1 that the act applies to Háskóli Íslands, Háskóli Íslands á Hólum as a member university of the Háskóli Íslands consortium, Háskólinn á Akureyri, and Landbúnaðarháskóli Íslands, “sem reknir eru sem opinberir háskólar og lúta yfirstjórn ráðherra.” A bare reference to the minister in that act means the Minister of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education → [[ministry-of-culture-innovation-and-higher-education]]. The other two separately listed public universities are [[haskolinn-a-akureyri]] and [[landbunadarhaskoli-islands]].

Article 8 says the minister appoints the university rector for five years on the nomination of the university council: “[Ráðherra] skipar háskólarektor til fimm ára samkvæmt tilnefningu háskólaráðs.” The same article says the rector chairs the university council and is the head of the university’s administration.

Article 24 says each university has its own appropriation in the budget law and that the minister makes proposals for the contributions to each university to meet the costs of teaching, research and other tasks: “Hver háskóli hefur sjálfstæða fjárveitingu á fjárlögum. Ráðherra gerir tillögur um fjárframlög til hvers háskóla.” The same article caps registration fees at 100.000 kr. per student per year.

The 2026 budget report for policy area 21 says the activity of subcategory 21.10 is in the hands of four public and three privately run universities, plus the Tilraunastöð Háskóla Íslands í meinafræði að Keldum research institute, the Stofnun rannsóknasetra Háskóla Íslands, and the regional research and knowledge centres. Its 2026 task table prints one line naming this university: “Uppbygging húss Heilbrigðisvísindasviðs HÍ með áherslu á betri innviði og umgjörð rannsókna og náms.” The funding column for that line prints “Innan ramma” → [[policy-area-21-haskolastig]] → [[treasury-budget-2026]].

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints the rows for policy area 21 at the subcategory level, “21.10 Háskólar og rannsóknastarfsemi”. It does not print a row naming this university, so no Article 4 row is recorded against it here.

The university’s own site prints the address Sæmundargötu 2, 102 Reykjavík and kennitala 600169-2039. Its faculty list includes Ferðamáladeild, Fiskeldis- og fiskalíffræðideild, and Hestafræðideild, the fields the budget report ties to Háskólinn á Hólum. The statute calls that school Háskóli Íslands á Hólum; the July 2026 budget report calls it Háskólann á Hólum. Hólar has no separate record here.
