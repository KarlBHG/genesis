# Skatturinn

## Summary
Stjórnarráðið's institutions list places Skatturinn under Fjármála- og efnahagsráðuneytið. This record therefore stores the ministry relationship as an authority line from [[ministry-of-finance]].

Skatturinn's own front page says the institution handles tax returns, tax assessment, and collection. The page opened for this pass prints no founding year, and no separate budget amount that can be stored as a source-bound money edge. The state treasury record remains [[rikissjodur]], and budget-law rows are captured at [[treasury-budget-2026]].
