# Fiskistofa

## Summary
Fisheries Iceland's management page says the ministry sets the total allowable catch and regulations, and that the Directorate of Fisheries then allots quotas. This record stores that split as a typed authority line from [[ministry-of-industries]] to Fiskistofa, and keeps quota mechanics on [[fisheries-and-quotas]].

The same government institutions roster used across this pass lists Fiskistofa under Atvinnuvegaráðuneytið. The entry therefore keeps both a sector source and a cabinet roster source for the same relationship.

No source opened in this pass printed a standalone annual amount for Fiskistofa that could be stored as an `amount` object without synthesis.
