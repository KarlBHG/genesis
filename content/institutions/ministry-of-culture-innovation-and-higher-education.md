# Menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðuneytið

## Summary
The English about page calls this the Ministry of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education. The Icelandic page is headed Menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðuneytið. The current-government page lists Logi Einarsson as Minister of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education and as Minister for Nordic Co-operation. The Icelandic ministry page says Logi took office as menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðherra on 21 December 2024. The English minister page says he has held both offices since 2024, without a day on the Nordic title.

The English page says the ministry oversees culture, media, innovation, science, research, higher education, language preservation, and cultural heritage.

Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints a total authorisation of 137.418,4 m.kr. for Menningar-, nýsköpunar- og háskólaráðuneyti → [[treasury-budget-2026]].
