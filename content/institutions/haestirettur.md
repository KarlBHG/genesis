# Hæstiréttur Íslands

## Summary
The consolidated Act on the courts, No. 50/2016, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, opens its first chapter on the court structure with Article 1: “Hæstiréttur Íslands er æðsti dómstóll ríkisins og hefur aðsetur í Reykjavík.” The appeal court below it is [[landsrettur]] and the eight courts of first instance are [[heradsdomstolar]].

Article 13 of the same act says seven judges sit in Hæstiréttur and that the President of Iceland appoints them for an indefinite term on the minister’s proposal: “Í Hæstarétti Íslands eiga sæti sjö dómarar sem forseti Íslands skipar ótímabundið samkvæmt tillögu ráðherra.” A bare reference to the minister in that act means the Minister of Justice → [[ministry-of-justice]]. Article 11 says the same minister appoints the five-member evaluation committee that assesses applicants for judicial office, with one member nominated by Hæstiréttur.

Article 7 says the budget bill and the budget law must set out separately the appropriations to Hæstiréttur Íslands, Landsréttur, the district courts jointly, and the judicial administration: “Í fjárlögum skal kveðið sundurliðað á um fjárveitingar til Hæstaréttar Íslands, Landsréttar, héraðsdómstólanna sameiginlega og dómstólasýslunnar.” Article 4 of the 2026 budget law prints two rows naming this court, one under Æðsta stjórn ríkisins and one under Dómsmálaráðuneyti. Those rows are not added together → [[policy-area-02-domstolar]].

Article 21 of the same act says Hæstiréttur judgments are published together with the decisions of Landsréttur and the district courts that belong with them. The shared administration of all three tiers is [[domstolasyslan]]. The index page for the judiciary as a whole is [[courts]].
