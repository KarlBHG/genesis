# Sýn hf.

## Summary
Sýn hf.’s 2024 consolidated statements say the company provides telecommunications, media, and technology services through the brands Vodafone, Stöð 2, Stöð 2+, Stöð 2 Sport, Vísir, Bylgjan, FM957, X977, and Já, and the subsidiaries Endor ehf. and Eignarhaldsfélagið Njála ehf. The same page says the company has a wide international partnership with Vodafone Group. Revenue from goods and services in 2024 was 21.647 millj. kr., against 21.746 millj. kr. in 2023. There were 380 shareholders at year-end 2024, against 367 in 2023.
