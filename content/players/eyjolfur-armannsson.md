# Eyjólfur Ármannsson

## Summary
The Minister of Infrastructure page says Eyjólfur assumed the office of Minister of Transport and Local Government on 21 December 2024, and became Minister of Infrastructure on 15 March 2025 when the title of the office was changed by presidential decree. His Alþingi CV says he has been a member of Alþingi for the Northwest constituency since 2021, for Flokkur fólksins → [[ministry-of-infrastructure]] → [[althingi]] → [[flokkur-folksins]].

The interests page, last changed 4 February 2025, says “Íslandsbanki hf. - keypt fyrir eina milljón króna vorið 2021.” → [[islandsbanki]]. The CV says he was a board member of the handball division of Víkingur in 2014–2015, and chair of Orkan okkar from 2020, with no end year printed → [[handknattleiksdeild-vikings]] → [[orkan-okkar]].
