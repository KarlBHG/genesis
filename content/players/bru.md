# Brú lífeyrissjóður

## Summary
Brú lífeyrissjóður’s 2025 annual accounts say amounts in the board report are in m.kr., million krónur. Net assets for pension payments were 624,405 m.kr. at year-end 2025, against 557,166 m.kr. at year-end 2024. The same report says total assets were 625,174 m.kr. at year-end 2025, against 557,817 m.kr. a year earlier. Those are two printed lines. This entry does not treat one as the other.

The net-assets line splits by division, in m.kr., at year-end 2025: A deild 399,859, V deild 95,648, R deild 98,367, E deild 14,552, and B deild 15,980. The report prints a total of 624,405 on that row.

Note 32 lists shareholdings whose stake is above 2% of issued share capital, or whose book value is above 5% of net assets for pension payments. Arion banki hf. is in that listed-share table at 5.33% on 31 December 2025, with a 2025 book value of 15,295,400. Íslandsbanki hf. is in the same table at 4.30%, with a 2025 book value of 11,914,791. The accounts say figures are in thousands of krónur unless stated otherwise. Landsbankinn and Landsvirkjun are not named in that share table. A later counterparty list that includes Landsbankinn hf. covers domestic equities and bonds together, so it is not recorded here as a shareholding → [[arion-banki]] → [[islandsbanki]].

The 2025 national summary is [[pension-savings-2025]]. Article 44 of the consolidated Act No. 129/1997 assigns supervision of pension funds to Fjármálaeftirlitið → [[financial-supervisory-authority]].
