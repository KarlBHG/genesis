# BHM

## Summary
BHM’s own about page says the federation is the umbrella organisation of 23 affiliated unions, that those unions have over 19 thousand members with varied backgrounds and specialist knowledge working across all fields of society, and that BHM guards the importance of ingenuity, specialist skill and education. The page says BHM members work in both the general and public markets. The office and service centre are at Borgartún 27, 105 Reykjavík, and the page prints kennitala 630387-2569. The member count is prose here, not an edge. The other peak federations with records here are [[asi]] and [[bsrb]]; the employers’ side is [[sa-confederation]].

Act No. 55/2006 on labour market measures, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 5 that the minister appoints a nine-member board of Vinnumálastofnun on nominations and that one member is nominated by Bandalag háskólamanna → [[vinnumalastofnun]].

Act No. 1/1997 on the Pension Fund for State Employees says in Article 6 that the fund’s board has eight members and that “stjórn Bandalags háskólamanna skipar einn stjórnarmann” → [[lsr]].

VIRK’s charter names Bandalag háskólamanna among the founders with kennitala 630387-2569, the same kennitala BHM prints on its own site, and a founding contribution of 62.500 kr. The charter says one of the seven board seats is filled jointly by BSRB, KÍ and BHM → [[virk-starfsendurhaefing]].
