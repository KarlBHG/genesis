# Vísir.is

## Summary
The media commission’s register entry for Vísir.is prints the outlet name Vísir.is and the media service provider “Sýn hf.” It names Herdís Dröfn Fjeldsted as the representative, Kolbeinn Tumi Daðason as the responsible editor for news on SÝN, Bylgjan and Vísir, and Kristjana Thors Brynjólfsdóttir as responsible for other programming. It prints the address Suðurlandsbraut 8, 108 Reykjavík, kennitala 470905 1740, and the website www.visir.is → [[syn]].

For ownership, the commission does not print a shareholder list. Its note says the ownership of listed media companies can change rapidly and even daily, and it refers instead to Sýn’s own largest-shareholders page. Sýn’s 2024 consolidated statements say the company provides telecommunications, media and technology services through the brands Vodafone, Stöð 2, Stöð 2+, Stöð 2 Sport, Vísir, Bylgjan, FM957, X977 and Já → [[telecoms]].

Torg ehf. is sometimes named alongside Vísir. The register and Sýn’s own statements put Vísir under Sýn. The company registry says Torg ehf. was ruled bankrupt on 4 April 2023 → [[torg]]. The other private news outlet with a record here is [[morgunbladid]].
