# Torg ehf.

## Summary
The company registry entry for Torg ehf., kennitala 6108081230, says the company was founded or registered on 14 August 2008 and prints “(Úrskurðað gjaldþrota 04.04.2023)”. The contact line names Óskar Sigurðsson as trustee. The postal address is Borgartúni 26, 105 Reykjavík and the legal domicile Kalkofnsvegi 2, 101 Reykjavík. The legal form is E1 Einkahlutafélag (ehf).

The VAT table on that entry prints registration number 129883 from 1 December 2017 to 4 April 2023 with the main activity 58130 Dagblaðaútgáfa, newspaper publishing, and a second number from 4 April 2023 with the main activity 63990. The annual accounts register shows filings as Torg ehf. for 2017 to 2021, as Torg vefverslun ehf. for 2012 to 2016, and as AB 365 ehf for 2010 and 2011. The beneficial owner row names Helgi Magnússon, born January 1949, resident and citizen of Iceland, type of holding “Bein stjórnun”.

The media commission’s library keeps Torg’s own rules on editorial independence, which say the news media of Torg ehf. shall uphold democratic fundamentals and free expression under the media act No. 38/2011, and that the board and managing director of Torg bear responsibility for the operation of the media service provider and the content it distributes.

Vísir.is is sometimes named alongside Torg. The media register puts Vísir under Sýn hf., not Torg → [[visir]]. The other private newspaper record here is [[morgunbladid]]. This record is marked prose-only: no current source names an outlet it still operates, so no typed edge is stored.
