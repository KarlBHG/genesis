# Logi Einarsson

## Summary
Logi Einarsson’s Alþingi CV says he has been Minister of Culture, Innovation and Higher Education since 21 December 2024 and Minister for Nordic Co-operation since 23 December 2024. It says he has been a member of Alþingi for the Northeast constituency since 2016, vice-chair of Samfylkingin in 2016 and chair from 2016 to 2022, and chair of the party’s parliamentary group from 2022 to 2025 → [[ministry-of-culture-innovation-and-higher-education]] → [[samfylkingin]] → [[althingi]].

The government biography says he has chaired the parliamentary group since 2022 and does not print an end year. The edge uses the CV range. The CV says he sat on the board of Arkitektafélag Íslands from 2010 to 2013 → [[arkitektafelag-islands]]. The interests page, last changed 17 March 2026, names Kiðhús ehf. and does not print a share, so that line is not an edge. Municipal seats on the CV are not drawn.
