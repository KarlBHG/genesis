# Þorbjörg Sigríður Gunnlaugsdóttir

## Summary
Þorbjörg Sigríður Gunnlaugsdóttir’s Alþingi CV says she has been Minister of Justice since 21 December 2024, and a member of Alþingi for Reykjavík North from 2020 to 2024 and for Reykjavík South since 2024, for Viðreisn → [[ministry-of-justice]] → [[althingi]] → [[vidreisn]].

The CV names board roles at Kvennaathvarfið, the Icelandic national committee of Unifem, and Bjarkarhlíð → [[kvennaathvarfid]] → [[unifem-island]] → [[bjarkarhlid]]. The interests page, last changed 18 March 2025, does not name a shareholding or a board seat.
