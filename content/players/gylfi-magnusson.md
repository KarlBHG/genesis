# Gylfi Magnússon

## Summary
Reykjavík Energy’s consolidated annual financial statements for 2024 name Gylfi Magnússon as Chair of the Board in the board photograph caption and list him first on the signed board line for the report dated 7 March 2025 → [[orkuveita-reykjavikur]]. The governance section titles him “Dr. Gylfi Magnússon, Chairperson” and says he has been chairperson since 2022. The passages used here do not print separate start or end dates for the chairmanship on the signature block.
