Akureyrarbær is a municipality in north Iceland. Hagstofa’s population table for 1 December 2024 prints 19,790 inhabitants. The association of municipalities is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Local government sits with [[ministry-of-infrastructure]].
