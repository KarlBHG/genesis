# Inga Sæland

## Summary
The Minister of Education and Children page says Inga assumed that office on 11 January 2026, and that she served as Minister of Social Affairs and Housing from 21 December 2024 to 11 January 2026. Her Alþingi CV says she founded Flokkur fólksins in 2016 and has been its chair since then, and that she has been a member of Alþingi for Reykjavík South since 2017 → [[ministry-of-education-and-children]] → [[ministry-of-social-affairs-and-housing]] → [[flokkur-folksins]] → [[althingi]].

The interests page, last changed 20 May 2021, says her holding in Icelandair was worth 1,238,462 kr. on that date, with a nominal value of 769,231 kr., and that it was bought in the spring of 2020 → [[icelandair]].
