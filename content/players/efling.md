# Efling stéttarfélag

## Summary
ASÍ’s member list names Efling, stéttarfélag among the unions under Starfsgreinasamband Íslands, the general workers’ federation inside ASÍ. The list prints Starfsgreinasamband Íslands at Guðrúnartúni 1, 105 Reykjavík with Vilhjálmur E. Birgisson as chair → [[asi]].

Efling’s own about page prints the address Guðrúnartúni 1, 105 Reykjavík, the phone 510 7500, the address efling@efling.is, and the office hours. That page does not print a member count, so none is recorded here.

Bjarg íbúðafélag says its flats are for people on the labour market who have been fully paid-up members of unions affiliated with ASÍ or BSRB for at least 16 of the last 24 months. Efling is one of those ASÍ unions on the ASÍ list. That eligibility rule is not an edge from this union → [[bjarg-ibudafelag]].
