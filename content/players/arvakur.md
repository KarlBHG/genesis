# Árvakur hf.

## Summary
The media commission’s register entry for Árvakur hf. lists Morgunblaðið and mbl.is as its registration-liable media and K100 and Retró 89,5 as its licence-liable media. It names Haraldur Johannessen as the company’s representative and as the responsible editor of Morgunblaðið and mbl.is, and Magnús E. Kristjánsson as responsible for K100 and Retró 89,5. The address is Hádegismóum 2, 110 Reykjavík, the phone 569 1100, and the kennitala 430169 1069 → [[morgunbladid]].

The same entry prints the ownership: “99% Þórsmörk ehf. kt. 440194 2149” and “1% Legalis sf. kt. 430191 1369”. It says the ownership information was last updated on 6 January 2026 and that the change to the registered responsible editor of Morgunblaðið and mbl.is was made on 2 March 2026 → [[thorsmork]].
