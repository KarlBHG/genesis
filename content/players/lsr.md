# Lífeyrissjóður starfsmanna ríkisins

## Summary
The English site calls LSR the Pension Fund for State Employees in Iceland. The history page says it is the oldest pension fund in the country, that a first proper pension fund was created in 1919, and that the fund marked a centenary in 2019. Act No. 101/1943, which the history page says entered into force in 1944, changed the name to the Pension Fund for State Employees. The page says members then included civil servants already covered and other employees paid by the Treasury, hired for a year or more, whose government work was their main job.

The English Overview 2024 says amounts are in millions of ISK. Net assets for pension payments at year-end 2024 were 1,566,743.9 million ISK. The division split is on [[lsr-net-assets-2024]]. This entry does not state a total for all pension funds in Iceland. The 2025 summary published by Fjármálaeftirlit Seðlabanka Íslands is [[pension-savings-2025]]. Article 44 of the consolidated Act No. 129/1997 assigns supervision of pension funds to Fjármálaeftirlitið → [[financial-supervisory-authority]].
