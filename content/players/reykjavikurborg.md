# Reykjavíkurborg

## Summary
Reykjavíkurborg is the capital municipality. Its 2024 financial statements say that on 1 December 2024 the number of inhabitants was 145.931, according to Þjóðskrá. The management overview says the figures are at each year’s prices, in milljónum kr. For the A and B sections together, operating revenue in 2024 is printed as 277.439.

The city’s page on the mayor says Hildur Björnsdóttir took office on 2 June 2026. The 2024 financial statements, signed while the previous majority was in office, name Heiða Björg Hilmisdóttir as mayor. The association of municipalities is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Local government is a responsibility of [[ministry-of-infrastructure]]. The city’s holding in the utility is [[orkuveita-reykjavikur]].
