# Arion banki

## Summary
Arion Bank’s 2025 report says the bank’s assets grew by 8% in 2025 and amounted to ISK 1,755 billion at year-end. An indicator table in the same report prints total assets of 1,756 billion ISK for 2025.

The same report says the capital ratio is above the requirements of the Financial Supervisory Authority of the Central Bank of Iceland → [[sedlabanki]] → [[financial-supervisory-authority]]. Its place among commercial banks after 2008 is [[banking-after-2008]].
