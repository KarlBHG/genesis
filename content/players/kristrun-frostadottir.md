# Kristrún Frostadóttir

## Summary
The current-government page says Kristrún Frostadóttir’s government took office on 21 December 2024 and lists her as Prime Minister. The parties named there are the Social Democratic Alliance, the Reform Party, and the People’s Party. The Prime Minister’s own page says she is the current Prime Minister, took office in December 2024, and leads a coalition of the Social Democratic Alliance, the Liberal Reform Party, and the People’s Party. That page uses “Liberal Reform Party” where the cabinet page says “Reform Party”.

The Prime Minister’s page says she has been chairman of the Social Democratic Alliance since 2022 and was elected to Althingi in 2021 for the Reykjavík South constituency. The legislature is [[althingi]]. The finance minister in the same cabinet is [[dadi-mar-kristofersson]].

The Prime Minister page’s HTML includes a render date of 27 September 2026 and still describes her as the current Prime Minister. Education and employment before parliament are on that page and are not repeated here.

The Alþingi CV says “Forsætisráðherra síðan 21. desember 2024.”, “Formaður Samfylkingarinnar síðan 2022.”, and “Alþingismaður Reykjavíkurkjördæmis suður síðan 2021 (Samfylkingin).” The header of that same CV says the constituency is Reykjavíkurkjördæmi norður. Both lines are printed. The interests page, last changed 4 June 2024, names AGR Dynamics ehf., Aurum ehf., and Munnbitinn ehf. without a share, so those lines are not edges → [[samfylkingin]].
