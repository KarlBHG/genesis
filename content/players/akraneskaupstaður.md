Akraneskaupstaður is a municipality on the west coast. Þjóðskrá’s spreadsheet published with the 10 December 2024 release prints 8,463 inhabitants on 1 December 2024. The utility [[orkuveita-reykjavikur]] lists Akraneskaupstaður among its municipal owners. The association of municipalities is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Local government sits with [[ministry-of-infrastructure]].
