# Icelandair

## Summary
Icelandair’s 2025 annual report prints total income of 1,740,886 in a table headed USD thousands, against 1,570,607 in 2024. The same report says total income amounted to USD 1,740.9 million, up 11% from the previous year, and calls that record income of USD 1.7 billion. It says the company significantly strengthened its position as the leading hub carrier at Keflavík airport. Isavia’s 2025 statements say it operates the country’s airports, and that its domicile is at Keflavíkurflugvöllur → [[isavia]].

One passage says Icelandair transported over 5 million of the total of 8 million who traveled through Keflavík airport during the year. A later line prints that Icelandair carried 5.1m of 8.1m passengers who traveled through Keflavík in 2025. The same report says strong connectivity underpins tourism development → [[tourism]].
