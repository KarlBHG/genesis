Kópavogsbær is a municipality in the capital area. Hagstofa’s population table for 1 December 2024 prints 40,258 inhabitants for Kópavogsbær. The association of municipalities is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Local government sits with [[ministry-of-infrastructure]].
