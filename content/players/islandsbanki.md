# Íslandsbanki

## Summary
Íslandsbanki’s 2025 Annual and Sustainability Report says the Icelandic State completed the sale of its remaining 45.2% shareholding in 2025 and that the bank moved fully into private ownership. The chief executive’s address in that report says the divestment was in May. The report says there were more than 25,000 shareholders at year-end 2025, and that the bank has been listed on Nasdaq Iceland since June 2021.

The same report names Jón Guðni Ómarsson as Chief Executive Officer and Heiðar Guðjónsson as Chairman of the Board → [[jon-gudni-omarsson]] → [[heidar-gudjonsson]]. It says the Icelandic State completed the sale. It does not, in the passages used here, name the ministry that sold the shares. This entry does not list private shareholders.

A chart in the report, labeled total assets in billion ISK, prints 1,728 at 31 December 2025.
