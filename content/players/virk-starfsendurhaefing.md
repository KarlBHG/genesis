# Virk starfsendurhæfing

## Summary
VIRK’s history page says Alþýðusamband Íslands and Samtök atvinnulífsins agreed a new arrangement for vocational rehabilitation in Iceland in the February 2008 collective agreements, that the self-owning institution VIRK Starfsendurhæfingarsjóður was founded on 19 May and began operating in August 2008, and that public employers and public employees’ unions joined as founders at the start of 2009. It says VIRK thereby became the first institution that all the main labour market parties stood behind together. The page says Act No. 60/2012 on work-related vocational rehabilitation was passed by Alþingi in 2012 and that under it VIRK’s role is to strengthen the working capacity of individuals with impaired health who aim at increased participation in the labour market.

VIRK’s charter names the founders with their kennitölur: Alþýðusamband Íslands 420169-6209, Samtök atvinnulífsins 680699-2919, BSRB 440169-0159, Kennarasamband Íslands 501299-3329, Bandalag háskólamanna 630387-2569, the finance minister on behalf of the Treasury 550169-2829, Reykjavíkurborg 530269-7609, and Samband íslenskra sveitarfélaga 550269-4739. It says financial obligations of the fund beyond the founding contribution are no concern of the founders → [[asi]] → [[sa-confederation]] → [[bsrb]] → [[bhm]].

The charter fixes the board: “Stjórn VIRK - Starfsendurhæfingarsjóðs er skipuð sjö fulltrúum og sjö fulltrúum til vara. Nánar tiltekið tveimur frá ASÍ, einum frá BSRB, KÍ og BHM sameiginlega, tveimur frá SA, einum frá fjármála- og efnahagsráðuneyti, Sambandi íslenskra sveitarfélaga og Reykjavíkurborg sameiginlega, og framkvæmdastjóra Landssamtaka lífeyrissjóða fyrir hönd lífeyrissjóða.” It says board members are appointed for one year at a time and that the chair rotates on a six-year cycle: two years with ASÍ, two with SA, one with the public employees’ unions and one with the public employers. The founding contributions it prints are ASÍ 500.000, SA 500.000, BSRB 125.000, KÍ 62.500, BHM 62.500, and the finance minister 125.000.

The fund’s 2026 annual meeting notice prints the address Borgartúni 18, 105 Reykjavík and kennitala 440608-0510, and lists “Tilkynning um skipan stjórnar VIRK 2026-2027” on the agenda.

Ragnar Þór Ingólfsson’s page on the Alþingi site says “Í stjórn Virk starfsendurhæfingar 2017–2023.” That page does not give an address → [[ragnar-thor-ingolfsson]].
