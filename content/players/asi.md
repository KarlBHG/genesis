# ASÍ

## Summary
ASÍ’s own about page says Alþýðusamband Íslands is a confederation of unions of wage earners on the general labour market, founded in 1916, and that it is the largest mass movement of wage earners in the country. The page says the confederation was founded on 12 March 1916 and prints the counts 44 aðildarfélög, 5 landssambönd, and 140k félagar. Those counts are prose here, not edges. The page also says the framework around ASÍ’s role is its own rules, which authorise the annual meeting and the central board to set regulations.

ASÍ’s member list names the landssambönd and their unions. Efling, stéttarfélag appears under Starfsgreinasamband Íslands, and Landssamband íslenzkra verzlunarmanna appears with its own block at Kringlunni 7 → [[efling]] → [[landssamband-verslunarmanna]]. LÍV’s own page says LÍV is a member of ASÍ. VR’s 2025 annual accounts say VR is a member of LÍV and that LÍV in turn is a member of ASÍ → [[vr]].

Act No. 55/2006 on labour market measures, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 5 that the minister appoints a nine-member board of Vinnumálastofnun for four years at a time on nominations and that two of those members are nominated by Alþýðusamband Íslands → [[vinnumalastofnun]].

VIRK’s charter names Alþýðusamband Íslands first among the founders, with kennitala 420169-6209 and a founding contribution of 500.000 kr., and says two of the seven board seats come from ASÍ. VIRK’s history page says ASÍ and Samtök atvinnulífsins agreed a new arrangement for vocational rehabilitation in the February 2008 collective agreements and that the self-owning institution was founded on 19 May that year → [[virk-starfsendurhaefing]] → [[sa-confederation]].

BSRB’s page on Bjarg says Bjarg íbúðafélag is a housing self-owning institution founded by BSRB and Alþýðusamband Íslands and that both put up the founding capital → [[bjarg-ibudafelag]] → [[bsrb]]. The graduate professionals’ federation is [[bhm]].

Ragnar Þór Ingólfsson’s page on the Alþingi site says “Í miðstjórn og varaforseti ASÍ 2017–2024.” That page does not give an address → [[ragnar-thor-ingolfsson]].
