# Þorgerður Katrín Gunnarsdóttir

## Summary
Þorgerður Katrín Gunnarsdóttir’s Alþingi CV says she has been Minister for Foreign Affairs since 21 December 2024, chair of Viðreisn since 2017, and a member of Alþingi across the periods that same sentence lists, from Reyknesinga in 1999–2003 through the Southwest constituency since 2016 → [[ministry-for-foreign-affairs]] → [[vidreisn]] → [[althingi]].

The same CV lists Minister of Education from 31 December 2003 to 1 February 2009, and Minister of Fisheries and Agriculture from 11 January to 30 November 2017. Those ministries are not current entries, so they are not drawn. The interests page, last changed 3 February 2020, says she sits on the board of Golfsamband Íslands. That body is not an entry here.
