# Alcoa Fjarðaál

## Summary
Alcoa Fjarðaál’s 2025 community report says the smelter at Reyðarfjörður started aluminium production at the beginning of April 2007 and reached full capacity in August 2008. It says the operating permit authorises up to 360 thousand tonnes of aluminium a year. The permit named on that page was issued on 13 December 2024 and runs until 2040. Staff numbered 574 in 2025.

The same page prints two 2025 potroom figures. The opening paragraph says the potroom produced 351,059 tonnes and the casthouse shipped 351,564 tonnes. A later potroom section says the potroom produced 351,564 tonnes. Both sentences are on the page. This entry does not choose between them.

The 2024 community report says production that year was 340,826 tonnes of aluminium, and that the potroom figure was lower than planned because Landsvirkjun curtailed power in the winter of 2023–2024. It says casthouse shipments were 343,540 tonnes, and staff numbered 570.

Alcoa Corporation’s 2025 annual report, as of 31 December 2025, prints Fjarðaál’s nameplate capacity and Alcoa’s consolidated share of that capacity both as 351 thousand metric tonnes per year. The 2025 community report describes Alcoa Corporation as the parent company. The annual report does not print a separate revenue or asset figure for the Icelandic smelter.

The same annual report says Landsvirkjun supplies the smelter with electricity from a hydroelectric facility under a 40-year power contract that expires in 2048, with a price renegotiation effective from 2028 → [[landsvirkjun]] → [[landsvirkjun-power-sales]]. The national export line does not split aluminium by smelter → [[aluminium-exports-2024]] → [[power-and-aluminium]].
