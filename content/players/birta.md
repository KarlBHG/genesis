# Birta lífeyrissjóður

## Summary
Birta lífeyrissjóður’s 2025 annual report says net assets of the mutual division for pension payments were 707,878 million ISK at year-end, against 669,235 million ISK the year before. The personal division was 24,256 million ISK, against 22,952 million ISK. The specified personal division was 4,520 million ISK, against 3,920 million ISK. The report then prints the combined figure: assets of the three divisions together were 736,653 million ISK at year-end 2025, against 696,108 million ISK at year-end 2024. The report prints “736.653 millj. kr.” This entry uses that sentence. It does not add the three division lines again.

Note 8’s table of listed domestic shares says amounts are in thousands of krónur. Arion banki hf. is listed with an ownership stake of 2.8%. The 2025 column prints 8,014,844 and the 2024 column prints 7,556,043. Íslandsbanki hf. is listed at 1.6%. The 2025 column prints 4,412,163 and the 2024 column prints 3,771,758. Landsbankinn and Landsvirkjun are not in that table → [[arion-banki]] → [[islandsbanki]].

The 2025 national summary is [[pension-savings-2025]]. Article 44 of the consolidated Act No. 129/1997 assigns supervision of pension funds to Fjármálaeftirlitið → [[financial-supervisory-authority]].
