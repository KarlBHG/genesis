# Landspítali

## Summary
Landspítali’s history line describes the institution as a modern university hospital. The 2024 budget note on hospital services says specialized hospital services are in the hands of the health ministry, Landspítali, and Sjúkrahúsið á Akureyri → [[healthcare]] → [[ministry-of-health]].

The government release of 20 December 2023 says appropriations for construction of the new Landspítali in the 2024 budget are 19.3 billion ISK, and calls that project the largest single public investment to date. That figure is the construction appropriation in the budget, not an operating revenue for the hospital. Alþingi approved that budget → [[althingi]].
