# Bjarg íbúðafélag

## Summary
Bjarg’s own about page says: “Bjarg íbúðafélag er húsnæðissjálfseignarstofnun stofnuð af ASÍ og BSRB. Félagið er rekið án hagnaðarmarkmiða og er ætlað að tryggja tekjulágum fjölskyldum á vinnumarkaði aðgengi að öruggu íbúðarhúsnæði í langtímaleigu.” The page prints the address Kletthálsi 1, 110 Reykjavík, kennitala 490916-0670, and names Björn Traustason as managing director. It lists the board as Finnbjörn A. Hermansson as chair with Þorbjörn Guðmundsson, Magnús Már Guðmundsson, Sólveig Anna Jónsdóttir and Gundega Jaunlinina as members → [[asi]] → [[bsrb]].

The front page says the company operates under the act on general flats, that applicants must be members of a union inside ASÍ or BSRB, and that a tenant must have been active on the labour market and a fully paid-up member of an ASÍ or BSRB affiliate for at least 16 of the last 24 months. It says the first flats were handed over to tenants in June 2019.

BSRB’s own page on Bjarg says BSRB and ASÍ have put up the founding capital for the company and that the member unions of BSRB and ASÍ have granted it subordinated loans to secure its operating finance in the first years. That page also prints early building agreements with Reykjavíkurborg for 1,000 flats, Hafnarfjarðarbær for 150 and Akureyrarbær for 75, and says the 500th flat was handed over in autumn 2021. Those totals are the federation’s own account of the programme → [[construction-and-housing]].

VR’s 2025 annual accounts say VR owns VR Blær leigufélag hf., whose aim is building rental flats for members in cooperation with Bjarg íbúðafélag → [[vr]].

Ragnar Þór Ingólfsson’s page on the Alþingi site says “Í stjórn Bjargs íbúðafélags síðan 2017, varaformaður 2020–2024, formaður síðan 2024.” The board list Bjarg prints now names Finnbjörn A. Hermansson as chair. That page does not give an address → [[ragnar-thor-ingolfsson]].
