# Ministry of Industries

## Summary
The current-government page says Kristrún Frostadóttir’s government took office on 21 December 2024 and lists Hanna Katrín Friðriksson as Minister of Industries. The fisheries topic on government.is is headed Ministry of Industries.

Fisheries Iceland’s management page, which still says “Ministry of Industries and Innovations”, says that ministry sets the TAC and issues fisheries regulations. The Directorate of Fisheries then allots quotas → [[fisheries-and-quotas]] → [[fisheries-iceland]].

The tourism topic page is also headed Ministry of Industries, while its body still names the Ministry of Culture and Business Affairs → [[tourism]]. This entry does not treat those two ministry names as the same office.
