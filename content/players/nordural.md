# Norðurál Grundartangi

## Summary
Norðurál Grundartangi’s 2024 annual report says operating revenue was USD 793 million, compared with USD 827 million in 2023.

The same report says power is supplied to Grundartangi under long-term purchase agreements with three suppliers: HS, Landsvirkjun, and OR → [[landsvirkjun]] → [[power-and-aluminium]].

Landsvirkjun’s notice of 24 March 2026 says the companies signed a further agreement for Grundartangi, from the beginning of 2027 through the end of 2033, for up to 250 MW a year. The notice says the first agreement was signed in 1997 for approximately 100 MW, and that sales in recent years have exceeded 200 MW, more than one-third of the smelter’s energy demand → [[landsvirkjun-power-sales]].
