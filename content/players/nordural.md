# Norðurál Grundartangi

## Summary
Norðurál Grundartangi’s 2024 annual report says operating revenue was USD 793 million, compared with USD 827 million in 2023.

The same report says power is supplied to Grundartangi under long-term purchase agreements with three suppliers: HS, Landsvirkjun, and OR → [[landsvirkjun]] → [[power-and-aluminium]]. National aluminium export value, which this report does not split out, is [[aluminium-exports-2024]].
