# Alma D. Möller

## Summary
The Ministry of Health page says Alma D. Möller has served as Minister of Health since 21 December 2024. It also says she has been a member of parliament for the Southwest constituency since 2024, for the Social Democratic Alliance → [[ministry-of-health]] → [[althingi]].

The page does not say who appointed her, and it does not give a budget or a staff count for the minister. Health spending in the 2024 budget is on [[healthcare]].
