# Samtök atvinnulífsins

## Summary
The English site of Samtök atvinnulífsins uses the name Confederation of Icelandic Enterprise and the short name SA. It says SA is a service organization for Icelandic businesses, with six associations by industrial activity, and that over 2,000 companies are members. A business joins SA by joining one of those six associations.

The page lists the six as Samtök orku- og veitufyrirtækja, Samtök verslunar og þjónustu, Samtök fyrirtækja í sjávarútvegi, Samtök fyrirtækja í fjármálaþjónustu, Samtök ferðaþjónustunnar, and Samtök iðnaðarins. The fisheries association is [[fisheries-iceland]]. SA says it is the spokesperson for its member companies toward government and unions, negotiates collective agreements when members have given it that authority, and is a member of BUSINESSEUROPE.

The same page states a share of salaried employees working for member associations. It does not date that share, so the figure is left out. The page itself does not give an as-of date.
