# ISAL (Rio Tinto á Íslandi)

## Summary
Rio Tinto’s ISAL page says the smelter at Straumsvík began operations in 1969, with a capacity of 33,000 tons, and that it produces approximately 210,000 tonnes of aluminium billets. The page labels that 210 kt figure as 2025 figures. It says the smelter is operated by Rio Tinto Iceland Ltd as part of Rio Tinto Aluminium, and that 100% of its electricity is supplied by Landsvirkjun, the national power company → [[landsvirkjun]].

The Environment Agency’s permit page for Rio Tinto, Straumsvík, says the permit covers Rio Tinto á Íslandi hf. and authorises production of up to 230,000 tonnes of aluminium a year in the potrooms, plus the casthouse and related shops. The same page lists a decision amending the permit, dated 29 August 2024. The permit figure is an authorisation. It is not the 2025 output on Rio Tinto’s page.

Landsvirkjun’s notice of 15 February 2021 says the companies amended the power purchase agreement dating from 2010. The notice says the contract, as before, specifies the sale of 390 MW or “3.416 GWh pr. year” and expires in 2036. This entry quotes that energy figure as the notice prints it → [[landsvirkjun-power-sales]].

No annual accounts opened for this entry print revenue or assets. The national aluminium export line does not split the 33% by smelter → [[aluminium-exports-2024]] → [[power-and-aluminium]].
