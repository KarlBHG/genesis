Sveitarfélagið Árborg is a municipality in South Iceland. Þjóðskrá’s spreadsheet published with the 10 December 2024 release prints 12,330 inhabitants on 1 December 2024. The association of municipalities is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Local government sits with [[ministry-of-infrastructure]].
