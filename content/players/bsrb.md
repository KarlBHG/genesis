# BSRB

## Summary
BSRB’s own about page says it is the largest organisation of public employees in Iceland, that any union of employees in public service with the state, local authorities or on the general labour market can join, that the federation was founded on 14 February 1942, and that its role is to lead the interest and rights struggle of public-service employees toward employers and government. The page says the federation was previously called Bandalag starfsmanna ríkis og bæja and that the name was changed to BSRB by an amendment to its own rules in 2015. Its headquarters are the Félagamiðstöðin at Grettisgata 89.

The same site prints two different counts of affiliated unions and members on two pages, and a third on the English page. Those sentences are kept side by side in `data/contradictions.json` rather than resolved here. The other two peak federations with records here are [[asi]] and [[bhm]]; the employers’ side is [[sa-confederation]].

Act No. 55/2006 on labour market measures, in the statute collection dated 1 September 2026, says in Article 5 that the minister appoints a nine-member board of Vinnumálastofnun for four years at a time on nominations, and that one board member is nominated by BSRB: “Tveir fulltrúar í stjórn Vinnumálastofnunar skulu tilnefndir af Alþýðusambandi Íslands, einn af Bandalagi háskólamanna, einn af BSRB, einn af því ráðuneyti er fer með starfsmannamál ríkisins, einn af Sambandi íslenskra sveitarfélaga og tveir af Samtökum atvinnulífsins.” → [[vinnumalastofnun]]

Act No. 1/1997 on the Pension Fund for State Employees says in Article 6 that the fund’s board has eight members, that the minister appoints four, and that “stjórn Bandalags starfsmanna ríkis og bæja skipar tvo stjórnarmenn” → [[lsr]].

VIRK’s charter names BSRB as one of the founders, with kennitala 440169-0159 and a founding contribution of 125.000 kr., and says one of the seven board seats is filled jointly by BSRB, KÍ and BHM → [[virk-starfsendurhaefing]].

BSRB’s own page on Bjarg says Bjarg íbúðafélag is a housing self-owning institution founded by BSRB and Alþýðusamband Íslands, that BSRB and ASÍ have put up the founding capital, and that the member unions of BSRB and ASÍ have granted the housing company subordinated loans to secure its operating finance in the first years → [[bjarg-ibudafelag]].
