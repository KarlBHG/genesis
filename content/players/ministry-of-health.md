# Ministry of Health

## Summary
The Ministry of Health page names Alma D. Möller as Minister of Health since 21 December 2024, and says she has been a member of parliament for the Southwest constituency since 2024, for the Social Democratic Alliance → [[alma-d-moller]].

The government release of 20 December 2023, on the 2024 budget approved by Alþingi, says contributions to health affairs are about 379.4 billion ISK. That is the spending figure used here. It is the budget line for health affairs, not a figure for the ministry’s own office → [[healthcare]] → [[althingi]].

The 2024 hospital-services note says that policy area is the minister’s responsibility, and that specialized hospital services are carried by this ministry, Landspítali, and Sjúkrahúsið á Akureyri → [[landspitali]].

The government’s institutions list places Embætti landlæknis under Heilbrigðisráðuneytið → [[embaetti-landlaeknis]].
