# Nova

## Summary
Nova Klúbburinn hf.’s 2024 consolidated statements say it is the parent of Nova hf. and that its main activity is telecommunications and related operations. The statements cover Nova Klúbburinn hf. and the subsidiaries Nova hf. and NL9 ehf. Total revenue in 2024 was 13.314 milljónum króna, against 12.993 milljónir kr. the year before. At year-end 2024 there were about 1.700 shareholders, against about 2.300 at the start of the year.

The board report says the ten largest shareholders together held 65,15% at year-end 2024, against 59,17% at year-end 2023. Names printed in that list include Birta lífeyrissjóður, Lífeyrissj. starfsm. rík. A-deild og B-deild, Landsbréf hf., Stefnir hf., Stapi lífeyrissjóður, Lífsverk lífeyrissjóður, Festa lífeyrissjóður, Lífeyrissjóður verslunarmanna, and Íslandsbanki.
