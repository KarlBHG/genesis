# Frímúrarareglan á Íslandi

## Summary
Frímúrarareglan á Íslandi prints its office at Bríetartún 3, 105 Reykjavík. The same page says the order was founded on 23 July 1951, and that the first lodge in Iceland was founded on 6 January 1919. It describes the order as an independent association of men, with mannrækt as its aim, answering to no authority except the lawful authorities of Iceland.

This entry names no members, and it draws no link to a company, a ministry, or a public office.
