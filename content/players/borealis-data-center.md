# Borealis Data Center

## Summary
Landsvirkjun’s notice of 23 June 2026 says Borealis Data Center signed an agreement for an additional 12 MW of firm power, in addition to existing contracts, to support its data centre in Blönduós. The notice says Borealis has been a Landsvirkjun customer since it began operations in Iceland in 2018 → [[landsvirkjun]].

This entry uses only that notice. It does not describe ownership of Borealis.
