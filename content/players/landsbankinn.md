# Landsbankinn

## Summary
The 2025 factbook says Landsbankinn hf. is the largest financial institution in Iceland, was established on 7 October 2008, and that its predecessor’s history runs back to 1886. The fact sheet dated 31 December 2025 says the Icelandic State holds 98.2% of the shares, with approximately 819 shareholders, and names Lilja Björk Einarsdóttir as CEO → [[lilja-bjork-einarsdottir]]. The 2025 consolidated financial statements name [[jon-thorvardur-sigurgeirsson]] as Chairman on the board signature line.

The same fact sheet lists total assets of 2,324,939 million ISK at 31 December 2025, shown as 15,812 million EUR. It also lists 930 employees on that date.

The ownership page says the National Treasury is the largest owner of a 98.2% share and that the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs controls the State’s holding → [[ministry-of-finance]]. The holding is recorded at [[landsbankinn-state-holding]]. The bank’s place in the post-2008 system is [[banking-after-2008]] and [[2008-financial-crisis]].
