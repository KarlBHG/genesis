# Landsbankinn

## Summary
The 2025 factbook says Landsbankinn hf. is the largest financial institution in Iceland, was established on 7 October 2008, and that its predecessor’s history runs back to 1886. The fact sheet dated 31 December 2025 says the Icelandic State holds 98.2% of the shares, with approximately 819 shareholders, and names Lilja Björk Einarsdóttir as CEO → [[lilja-bjork-einarsdottir]].

The ownership page says the National Treasury is the largest owner of a 98.2% share and that the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs controls the State’s holding → [[ministry-of-finance]]. The holding is recorded at [[landsbankinn-state-holding]]. The bank’s place in the post-2008 system is [[banking-after-2008]] and [[2008-financial-crisis]].
