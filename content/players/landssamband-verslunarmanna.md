# Landssamband verslunarmanna

## Summary
LÍV’s own page says Landssamband íslenzkra verzlunarmanna was founded on 2 June 1957, that it is the umbrella organisation of staff in retail, office and service trades, and that it contains ten commercial employees’ unions and commercial divisions around the country. The page says the federation’s purpose is to be the joint platform of its member unions, to be their spokesperson, and to conduct collective bargaining as the member unions entrust to it from time to time. It adds that the LÍV office has conducted collective bargaining for the member unions other than VR → [[vr]] → [[retail]].

The same page says LÍV is a member of ASÍ. It describes ASÍ as founded in 1916 and says seven landssambönd of unions with about 70 thousand members belong to it → [[asi]].

That page states its own rank inside ASÍ twice and differently in the same text, once as the largest landssamband and once as the second largest. Both sentences are kept in `data/contradictions.json` rather than resolved here.

VR’s 2025 annual accounts say VR is a member of LÍV, that VR runs LÍV under contract, and that all of LÍV’s income and expenses are part of VR’s operations.

Ragnar Þór Ingólfsson’s page on the Alþingi site says “Í stjórn Landssambands verslunarmanna 2017–2024, formaður 2019–2024.” That page does not give an address → [[ragnar-thor-ingolfsson]].
