# Brim

## Summary
Brim’s results for 2024, published on 27 February 2025, say operating revenue for the year was 389.4 million euros, compared with 437.2 million euros in 2023.

The same statement says Icelandic authorities allowed no capelin season in 2024 and limited how much of the company’s cod quota it could catch in the Barents Sea. The fisheries minister in that account is the office this map records at [[ministry-of-industries]]. The quota system is [[fisheries-and-quotas]]. Marine-product exports for the country, not for this company, are [[marine-products-exports-2024]].
