# Jón Guðni Ómarsson

## Summary
Íslandsbanki’s 2025 Annual and Sustainability Report titles Jón Guðni Ómarsson as Chief Executive Officer. His address in that report says that in May the Icelandic State completed the divestment of its remaining stake → [[islandsbanki]].

The report does not date a separate appointment line for the office. The as-of year is the report year.
