# Síminn

## Summary
Síminn hf.’s 2024 consolidated financial statements say the company’s history dates to 1906 and that it has since been a mainstay of telecommunications services in Iceland. Total revenue for 2024 amounted to ISK 27.832 million, against ISK 25.741 million in 2023. At year-end 2024 there were 931 shareholders, against 1.027 at the beginning of the year, and two shareholders owned over 10% of the share capital. The names in the ten-largest table were not readable in the text that was opened, so this entry does not name them.
