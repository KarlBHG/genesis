# VR

## Summary
VR’s own about page says the union was founded in 1891 by employees and employers in the commercial trade as Verzlunarmannafélag Reykjavíkur, that it became a pure employees’ union on 28 February 1955, and that the name was changed to VR at the annual meeting on 26 April 2006. It says the purpose of the union is to work for better terms and increased rights for its members, and names Halla Gunnarsdóttir as chair.

VR’s 2025 annual accounts say: “VR er aðili Landssambandi íslenzkra verzlunarmanna (LÍV) en það er aðili að ASÍ sem er stærsta fjöldahreyfing launafólks á Íslandi.” The same report says “VR rekur samkvæmt samningi Landsamband Íslenzkra verzlunarmanna (LÍV). Allar tekjur og gjöld LÍV eru hluti af rekstri VR.” → [[landssamband-verslunarmanna]] → [[asi]]

The same report says VR belongs to two vocational training funds: Starfsmenntasjóður verslunar- og skrifstofufólks, which operates on a collective agreement between Samtök atvinnulífsins on one side and VR and LÍV on the other, and Starfsmenntasjóður verslunarinnar, which operates on a collective agreement between VR and LÍV on one side and Félag atvinnurekenda on the other. Those funds have no record here → [[sa-confederation]]. The report also says VR owns the company VR Blær leigufélag hf., whose aim is building rental flats for members in cooperation with Bjarg íbúðafélag → [[bjarg-ibudafelag]].

The report says the union’s area covers the jurisdictions of Reykjavík, Kópavogur, Hafnarfjörður, Garðabær, Seltjarnarnes, Mosfellsbær, Kjósarhreppur, Reykjanesbær, Grindavíkurbær, Suðurnesjabær, Vogar, Vestmannaeyjar, Árnessýsla, Rangárvallasýsla, Vestur-Skaftafellssýsla, Húnaþing vestra, Akranes, Hvalfjarðarsveit, Langanesbyggð, Vopnafjarðarhreppur, and Múlaþing. Its members work in retail, service and office trades → [[retail]].

Ragnar Þór Ingólfsson’s page on the Alþingi site says “Í stjórn VR 2009–2024, formaður stjórnar 2017–2024.” That page does not give an address → [[ragnar-thor-ingolfsson]].
