# Stapi lífeyrissjóður

## Summary
Stapi lífeyrissjóður’s 2025 annual accounts say the figures in the statements are in thousands of krónur. The balance sheet prints net assets for pension payments of 462,081,122 at year-end 2025, against 430,524,301 at year-end 2024. The same statement prints total assets of 462,224,657 at year-end 2025, against 430,918,956 a year earlier.

The board report states the divisions in m.kr., million krónur. Net assets of the insurance division for pension payments were 450,370 m.kr., up 7.3% from the year before. Net assets of the personal division were 10,397 m.kr., up 7.2%. Net assets of the specified personal division were 1,314 m.kr. at year-end. Those division lines are quoted as printed. They are not added here to the balance-sheet total.

The 2025 national summary is [[pension-savings-2025]]. Article 44 of the consolidated Act No. 129/1997 assigns supervision of pension funds to Fjármálaeftirlitið → [[financial-supervisory-authority]].
