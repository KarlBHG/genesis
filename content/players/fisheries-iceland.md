# Samtök fyrirtækja í sjávarútvegi

## Summary
Fisheries Iceland, Samtök fyrirtækja í sjávarútvegi, says it was established on 31 October 2014 when the Federation of Icelandic Fishing Vessel Owners and the Federation of Icelandic Fish Processing Plants merged. The about page says the goal is to safeguard the interests of the fisheries sector, including aquaculture companies.

Its fisheries-management page describes the quota system: the ministry decides the total allowable catch, and the Directorate of Fisheries allots quotas to vessels by their quota shares → [[fisheries-and-quotas]]. SA’s English page lists Samtök fyrirtækja í sjávarútvegi as one of its six member associations → [[sa-confederation]].

The about page also names a chief executive and an August 2016 start date. That person is not given a separate entry. The role is at an industry association, not a ministry or a listed company.
