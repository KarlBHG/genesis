# Isavia

## Summary
Isavia ohf. is a state-owned limited company. The 2025 consolidated financial statements say its purpose is aviation-related service activities and the operation and development of the country’s airports. Its domicile is at Flugstöð Leifs Eiríkssonar at Keflavíkurflugvöllur, and its venue is Hafnarfjörður. The same statements say the Icelandic State is the sole owner at the end of 2025, as at the end of 2024, and that the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs carries the voting rights on behalf of the Icelandic Treasury → [[ministry-of-finance]].

The statements say all amounts are in ISK thousands unless stated otherwise. Operating revenues in 2025 are printed as 49.261.589, against 51.917.233 in 2024. A separate sentence, which states its own unit, says revenue from the service agreement with the Ministry of Infrastructure for domestic airports and air navigation was ISK 3.094 million in 2025 → [[ministry-of-infrastructure]].

Icelandair’s 2025 report says it strengthened its position as the leading hub carrier at Keflavík airport → [[icelandair]].
