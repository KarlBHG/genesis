Reykjanesbær is a municipality on the Reykjanes peninsula. Þjóðskrá’s spreadsheet published with the 10 December 2024 release prints 24,313 inhabitants on 1 December 2024. The association of municipalities is [[samband-islenskra-sveitarfelaga]]. Local government sits with [[ministry-of-infrastructure]]. [[isavia]] operates Keflavík Airport in the municipality.
