# Morgunblaðið

## Summary
The media commission’s register entry for Morgunblaðið prints the outlet name Morgunblaðið and the media service provider “Árvakur hf.” It names Haraldur Johannessen as both the representative and the responsible editor, prints the address Hádegismóum 2, 110 Reykjavík, the phone 569 1100, kennitala 430169 1069, and the website www.mbl.is → [[arvakur]].

The register entry for Árvakur hf. lists Morgunblaðið and mbl.is as its registration-liable media and K100 and Retró 89,5 as its licence-liable media. It records the editorial policy as “Miðlun víðsýnnar umræðu, frétta og umfjöllun um margvísleg málefni” and notes that Árvakur’s rules on editorial independence are not confirmed by the commission, citing paragraph 3 of Article 24 of the media act No. 38/2011.

The only commission decision the register lists in Morgunblaðið’s matters is Ákvörðun 2/2016 on an unlawful alcohol advertisement in Morgunblaðið, dated 24 June 2016.

The register’s ownership block for Árvakur names Þórsmörk ehf. at 99 per cent → [[thorsmork]]. The comparable private news outlet with a record here is [[visir]].
