# ÍL-sjóður

## Summary
ÍL-sjóður’s half-year accounts to 30 June 2024 say the fund was created by Act No. 151/2019, in force on 31 December 2019. It handles the tasks, rights, duties, and assets of Íbúðalánasjóður that did not transfer to Húsnæðissjóður and Húsnæðis- og mannvirkjastofnun when Íbúðalánasjóður was dissolved. Its main role is winding those matters up. The same accounts say the Minister of Finance and Economic Affairs has oversight of ÍL-sjóður and of the settlement of the assets and liabilities the fund took over → [[ministry-of-finance]].

The statements say amounts are published in thousands of krónur unless stated otherwise. Loans, Útlán, are printed as 113.499.030 at 30 June 2024, against 117.873.809 at 31 December 2023. Total assets are printed as 546.182.153 at 30 June 2024. A separate sentence, which states its own unit, says the result from 1 January to 30 June 2024 was negative by 7.150 m.kr., and that equity at 30 June 2024 was negative by 261.378 m.kr. The accounts say the Treasury has a simple guarantee of the fund’s debts, that the fund has stopped making new loans, and that Húsnæðis- og mannvirkjastofnun administers the loan book.

Félags- og húsnæðismálaráðuneytið administers housing → [[ministry-of-social-affairs-and-housing]] → [[construction-and-housing]]. These accounts do not place ÍL-sjóður under that ministry.
