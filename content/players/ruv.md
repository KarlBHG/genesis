# Ríkisútvarpið

## Summary
Ríkisútvarpið ohf. is a public limited company. The 2025 consolidated financial statements say its main activity is public-service media, meant to meet democratic, cultural, and social needs in Iceland by transmitting text, sound, and images. At the end of 2025 there was one shareholder, as at its founding, and the company is owned by the Icelandic state.

The same statements say operating revenue in 2025 was 9.389 milljónum króna, against 9.149 milljónir króna in 2024. The legislature that sits alongside the state’s ownership of public institutions is [[althingi]].
