# Hoyvík Agreement

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says the Hoyvík Agreement between Iceland and the Faroe Islands entered into force on 1 November 2006. The same page says the agreement provides for the abolition of tariffs on all originating goods.

This entry does not print a separate fisheries quota regime. Marine exports are recorded at → [[marine-products-exports-2024]] and [[fisheries-and-quotas]].
