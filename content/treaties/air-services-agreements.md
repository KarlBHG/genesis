# Air services agreements

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says air services agreements (ASAs) provide for mutual authorizations for scheduled flights between contracting parties with or without stops in third countries. The page describes the freedoms of the air and says Iceland has concluded agreements that allow flights to over 120 countries.

Most agreements are bilateral. The page also names multilateral instruments, including the Air Transport Agreement with the United States covering Iceland, Norway, and the member states of the European Union, and the Agreement on the establishment of the European Common Aviation Area (ECAA), which covers Iceland, Norway, the member states of the EU, and Albania, among others on the same page.

The ministry says aviation is one of Iceland’s basic industries and that a strong ASA network promotes competition, fares, and Icelandic carriers’ access to international markets.
