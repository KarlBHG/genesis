# Investment protection index

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs maintains a list of investment agreements (updated June 2025). The page groups them by whether investment protection is in force.

**With investment protection in force — multilateral:** the EEA Agreement (in force 1 January 1994; parties include Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and EU member states); the EFTA Convention of 1960 (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland); and the EFTA–Singapore FTA.

**With investment protection in force — bilateral BITs:** China, Chile, Egypt, Korea, Lebanon, Mexico, and Viet Nam.

**Without investment protection in force:** EFTA FTAs with Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Indonesia, and Peru; bilateral Faroe Islands FTA.

**Not yet in force — bilateral BITs:** Georgia, Kuwait, North Macedonia, Turkey, Uganda, and Ukraine.

The page’s investment-agreements overview also names Chile, China, Egypt, Faroe Islands, India, Latvia, Lebanon, Lithuania, Mexico, Singapore, South Korea, Uganda, and Vietnam without assigning each to a protection category in the sentence used here.

**Other investment-related agreements (selection on the page):** Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency (MIGA); International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes (ICSID); UNCITRAL New York Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards (1958); WTO Agreement on Trade-Related Investment Measures (TRIMs) of 1994; Energy Charter Treaty of 1994.

The page says publication on the ministry website is for information only and does not replace publication in the Official Gazette of Iceland.
