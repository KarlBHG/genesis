# Schengen association

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs records that Iceland signed an association agreement on participation in the Schengen cooperation in December 1996, together with Denmark, Finland, Norway, and Sweden. The page describes Schengen as abolishing internal border checks, with compensatory police cooperation.

This map does not treat Schengen as a clause of the EEA Agreement. The EEA entry describes EEA membership as the setting that later enabled Schengen participation → [[eea-agreement]].

The ministry page used here does not give the day the association agreement was signed, or the date Schengen rules began to apply in Iceland. Those dates are left blank rather than filled in.
