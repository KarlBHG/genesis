# EFTA third-country FTAs

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs trade page says the EFTA states have developed a worldwide network of free trade agreements with countries outside the European Union, with a view to ensuring competitive and predictable conditions for companies and trade partners. Iceland negotiates many of these through EFTA rather than alone.

The ministry’s investment-agreements page (updated June 2025) groups several of those FTAs by investment-protection status:

- **Without investment protection in force:** EFTA FTAs with Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Indonesia, and Peru; bilateral Faroe Islands free trade agreement (see also → [[hoyvik-agreement]]).
- **Not yet in force:** EFTA FTAs with Malaysia and Thailand.
- **With investment protection in force (multilateral):** EFTA–Singapore FTA, listed alongside the EEA and EFTA Convention.

This entry records those government lists. It does not open each partner FTA as a separate map file unless a future pass adds one primary text per agreement.

Bilateral Iceland–China trade is separate → [[iceland-china-fta]]. The EFTA Convention itself is → [[efta]].
