# EFTA

## Summary
The Government of Iceland describes the EFTA Convention as an agreement among Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, and Switzerland. It says the convention was originally signed in 1960, that Iceland became a member in 1970, and that the revised EFTA Convention entered into force on 1 June 2002. It does not give the day in 1970. Switzerland remains outside the EEA → [[eea-agreement]].
