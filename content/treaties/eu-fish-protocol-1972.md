# Protocol 6 (1972 Iceland–EU fish preferences)

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs describes Protocol 6 to the free trade agreement between Iceland and the EU (then the EEC) from 1972. The page says it provides for tariff preferences for imports of Icelandic fish products into EU markets, and that those preferences remain in force where they grant better access than Protocol 9 to the EEA Agreement.

EEA fish tariff terms are recorded separately at → [[eea-agreement]] → [[fisheries-and-quotas]].
