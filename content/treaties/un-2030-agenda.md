# 2030 Agenda (SDGs)

## Summary
The Government of Iceland global-goals page says the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, adopted by all United Nations Member States in 2015, provides a shared blueprint for peace and prosperity. It says the 17 Sustainable Development Goals are an urgent call for action by all countries in a global partnership.

The page records Iceland’s Voluntary National Reviews to the United Nations in 2019 and June 2023, and describes domestic follow-up through Sustainable Iceland and stakeholder consultation. Development cooperation policy links the SDGs to Iceland’s aid spending → [[international-development-cooperation]].
