# Iceland–EU unprocessed agriculture (2018)

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says that, in addition to Protocol 3 to the EEA Agreement on processed agricultural products, a bilateral agreement has been in force between Iceland and the EU since 2018 on tariff preferences for unprocessed agricultural products. The page says that agreement replaced a 2007 agreement.
