# Iceland–China free trade agreement

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs trade page says the free trade agreement between Iceland and China entered into force on 1 July 2014. The page lists it among Iceland’s bilateral and plurilateral trade agreements alongside the EEA and EFTA frameworks → [[eea-agreement]] → [[china]].

Statistics Iceland’s 2024 goods-trade release names China among Iceland’s largest import partners. It does not attribute that ranking to this agreement alone → [[goods-imports-2024]].
