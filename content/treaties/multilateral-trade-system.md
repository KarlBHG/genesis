# Multilateral trading system

## Summary
The Ministry for Foreign Affairs says Iceland is a strong proponent of a strong, rules-based multilateral trading system and actively supports efforts to advance the role of the WTO. The same page says Iceland is party to numerous free trade agreements, plurilateral agreements, and bilateral agreements, and that the Directorate works with the Permanent Mission in Geneva on EFTA and WTO files.

This entry records that government description. It does not print the year Iceland joined the WTO or GATT on the page used here.

Bilateral and EFTA-channel agreements are listed separately on the treaties index → [[eea-agreement]] → [[efta]].
